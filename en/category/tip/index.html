    Free-time daily and weekly checklist

    Separate daily care, weekly training and social activities, and time-sensitive events when free time begins, then record completed tasks on this device.

    Weekly activities and discounts

    Choose weekly activities for skill training, recruitment, or battle preparation, using weekend discounts and each temple’s favorable day.

    Restore motivation before training

    Check the motivation of the companions you plan to train, then choose meals, gifts, theatre, baths or lodging by party needs and remaining uses. Compare costs, unlocks and turn consumption.

    Recruitment & support

    4 entriesView all

    First gifts and scouting

    Check recruitment in your current route, introduce yourself with a gift, then prepare support through weekly gifts and meals. Diagnose absence, event timing and negotiations when meeting the renown and support requirements is not enough.

    S supports and paired endings (spoilers)

    Separate support level from conversation rank, build your relationship with Eshmel, and check the reported requirements for an S scene and paired ending. The ending conditions come from a community translation of IGN and are folded into a spoiler section.

    Bargaining with Zarcone

    Meet Zarcone’s recruitment requirements, refuse to lower his fee, then accept at 10G. Refusing that offer ends negotiations.

    Skill training and spectating

    Plan weekly training around class and weapon requirements, then use spectating and inn training to fill the remaining skill EXP gap.

    Certification and seal stock

    Unlock certification, buy the right seal, check the pass rate, and change class. Separate renown gates from character requirements and prepare before spending limited seals.

    Cai’s lessons and class unlocks

    Find Cai’s instructors, collect lesson rewards, and prepare for certification after unlocking new classes.

    Organize exploration parties, restore travel facilities, and rebuild training and dining in Part III. Personal observations about level scaling are separated from the practical route.

    Part III Dagsion RNG observationsSpoilers

    A community experiment in Part III uses Eshmel’s trips into and out of Dagsion to change world-map outcomes. Separate procedures explain enemy placement, gathering retries and saving before a lure completes, including when autosaves change.

    Before retrying a fishing roll

    Understand the limits of a report about unchanged fishing results before repeatedly reloading, then plan your searches and reserve a Paradise Fish for Ninae.

    Saves & settings

    Chapter replay and saves

    Switching routes and replaying a chapter affect saves differently. Learn when suspended progress is lost, when chapter-start records are overwritten, and what you can return to.

    Cross-route carryover

    Separate save-wide achievements and Salvation’s Blessings, support between characters, and the map, side-quest and blessing carryover earned by completing a protagonist’s story. Plan the next route without confusing these benefits with renown.

    Japanese voice settings and first-launch limitations

    Find the Japanese voice setting under Extras on the title screen, and understand the first-launch, system-language and protagonist-name limitations.
