    How to read the table and heatmap

    Per-stat colors use each column’s mean and standard deviation across the full character roster. The mean is neutral; color saturates at ±2 standard deviations. Blue is below mean; red is above mean.

    Changing personal-skill context recalculates the baseline. Missing values are excluded; constant columns are neutral.

    Totals are simple sums of the nine rates, not unit rankings or a stat-gain probability. Growth modifiers are separate from immediate stat bonuses.

    Adds the higher of Strength or Magic growth to the other seven stats. Formula: total − min(Str, Mag).

    The skill option uses documented modified values only. Mu’s base rates and Signs of Growth rates stay separate, and Mu is counted once.
