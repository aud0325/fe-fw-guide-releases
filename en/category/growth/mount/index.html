    How to read the table and heatmap

    Classes and mounts share a fixed −30 to +30 pp scale for every stat. Negative values are blue, zero is white, positive values are red.

    Totals are simple sums of the nine rates, not unit rankings or a stat-gain probability. Growth modifiers are separate from immediate stat bonuses.
