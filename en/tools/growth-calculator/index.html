    Calculation rules

    Expected stats combine mean growth and fixed stat modifiers, rounded to whole numbers. Mount growths use maximum bond.

    Reference expected stats and percentiles use the selected starting level and training path. Changing the path updates both the reference values and assessment. Intrinsic stats are assessed after removing fixed class, mount, core, ability and battalion bonuses; displayed values use the target record’s modifiers. Minimum gains, promotion floors and caps are excluded.

    Average gains use intrinsic stats at the selected path’s starting level and the level-ups to the target. Fixed class, mount, core, ability and battalion bonuses are excluded. Expected average gains use growth rates along the same training path.
