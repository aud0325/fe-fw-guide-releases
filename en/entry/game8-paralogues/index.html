    Paralogue deadlines and scouting

    Plan around acceptance windows, completion deadlines and travel time. Find each route’s quest givers and dates, the easiest deadlines to miss, and recruitment conditions that remain after a paralogue.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide

    Plan acceptance, travel and completion separately i

    An acceptance window tells you when to obtain a quest; its completion deadline tells you when to finish it. Accepting does not preserve it indefinitely. Dates below are in-game Part I dates.

    1. Find your protagonist and chapter, then identify paralogues needed for your desired recruits. The character named in a paralogue is not necessarily your current protagonist.
    2. Prioritize the earliest acceptance or completion cutoff. Recheck the displayed quest deadline after accepting.
    3. Allow time to reach the paralogue and the main-story destination afterward. Starting a paralogue does not itself end free time, but travel still takes time.

    Deadline traps and conflicting dates iiiiii

    • On Dietrich’s route, accept Bertrand’s paralogue from Gabriel in Arena Square and finish on Sep 17. Treat it as that day’s priority before advancing to Sep 18.
    • Cai must accept Orchel’s paralogue by Oct 19; the separate completion deadline is Oct 21. Waiting until Oct 21 to obtain it is too late.
    • For Theodora, finish Dietrich’s and Orchel’s paralogues by the reported Oct 24 end of Chapter 12. Game8’s Nov 1 / Oct 27 listings do not establish access after that chapter ends.
    • Acceptance reports differ: Theodora’s paralogue, Game8 Sep 15 / RPG Site Sep 12; Anatolia’s, Oct 17 / Oct 14; Dietrich’s on Leda’s route, Nov 1 / Oct 22. Game8 is our acceptance reference; accepting before the earlier date avoids relying on the disputed interval.
    • RPG Site reports fixed battle dates of Sep 8 for Leda’s paralogue and Oct 22 for Cai’s. Accepting early and completing immediately are therefore different scheduling assumptions.

    Prepare the journey and find the battle location ii

    Visit and unlock useful stations before a distant trip, and reserve gold for fares. Select the carriage at a station; East and West Amalthea serve separate networks, so choose the correct side. Cai unlocks carriage travel through the Part I Chapter 7 favor about goods unavailable in the imperial capital; the other protagonists unlock it when Chapter 4 free time begins.

    The giver and battlefield need not be in the same place. For example, after obtaining Dietrich’s paralogue from Fabio, travel to the dark cave west of Dagsion and select the paralogue there. Read the accepted quest’s destination before leaving the giver.

    Check recruitment afterward · if you missed a window iiiii

    Completion may satisfy only one recruitment requirement. Dietrich’s Gaitz also requires Chapter 12. For Leda’s Fabio, Game8 lists Support 3, Renown 9 and story progress on Nov 4 in addition to completing Dietrich’s paralogue. An unavailable scouting option immediately after victory does not mean the paralogue failed.

    Talimun’s paralogue has a second acceptance window on Oct 1–12 for the same eligible routes. Do not assume other paralogues repeat. Chapter replay is an option for missed deadlines, but it is not a promise to preserve all current progress or training; review the save consequences before restarting.
