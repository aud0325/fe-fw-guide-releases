    Part I training guide

    Archery and exploration

    1. BeginnerHunter
    2. →SpecialtyRogue
    3. →AdvancedSniper

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop bow offense with exploration utility. Good speed and dexterity growth plus sword and bow proficiencies suit a Rogue stage before continued archery. Move within two spaces of allies who need pouch access.

    Skill availability Lv.20 Seize the Chance · Lv.35 Seize the Chance+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Choose Myrmidon and Shido for melee offense when exploration is covered. Bows offer safer range; swords favor higher speed growth.
