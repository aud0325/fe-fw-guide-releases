    Before retrying a fishing roll

    Understand the limits of a report about unchanged fishing results before repeatedly reloading, then plan your searches and reserve a Paradise Fish for Ninae.

    An individual observation, not a verified RNG rule.

    In this guide
    1. Before repeatedly reloading the same save
    2. Search Lake Brontes
    3. Before spending your first fish

    Before repeatedly reloading the same save ii

    The Raider King author reports unchanged results after immediate reloads and speculates that at least ten turns of search results are prepared in advance. This is an individual observation, not evidence that waiting ten turns changes the result or that another action guarantees the fish.

    If the same result keeps repeating, decide whether to spend more available turns exploring or return later instead of repeatedly reloading. The same guide offers sending a battalion to search the lake as an alternative on Theodora’s route; this is not a guaranteed Paradise Fish method either.

    Search Lake Brontes ii

    Paradise Fish is a rare fish. Game8 identifies Lake Brontes in the southern part of the world map as its gathering location. The lake also yields other fish, so an unsuccessful search does not mean you chose the wrong place.

    1. Locate Lake Brontes on the world map and travel to its exploration node. This guide concerns the search action at that lake.
    2. Search and inspect the rewards. Game8 lists Talto Goby, Ladon Loach, Ladon Pike, and Paradise Fish for this lake. Check the actual species and your inventory count.
    3. If it does not appear, check your remaining schedule before searching again: exploration spends turns. Game8 explains that PERFECT results change quantities and rarity and can yield fish hidden as ??? in the list. It does not guarantee a catch within a fixed number of searches.

    Before spending your first fish ii

    Recruiting Ninae requires one Paradise Fish in the negotiation, plus support 3 with the current protagonist and renown 6 for Cai or Theodora, or 8 for Dietrich or Leda. If you find the fish first, reserve one while preparing the other conditions so you do not have to hunt it again.

    Count Ninae’s fish separately from other uses. Game8 lists an exchange at the trading company in Alecto: one Paradise Fish for Giant’s Ring. Completing both recruitment and that exchange therefore requires two fish.

    It can also be used as an ingredient at a world-map feast or a dungeon rest. Feasts restore HP and spell uses and raise support. If recovery is your only goal, check your other ingredients before spending a rare fish reserved for recruitment or an exchange.
