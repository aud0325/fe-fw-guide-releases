    Chapter replay and saves

    Switching routes and replaying a chapter affect saves differently. Learn when suspended progress is lost, when chapter-start records are overwritten, and what you can return to.

    In this guide
    1. Distinguish current progress from chapter-start records
    2. Choose your purpose and return point before replaying
    3. Example: replaying Chapter 4 from the middle of Chapter 5
    4. Replay and carryover answer different questions
    5. Reported support repetition: what the player did
    6. What retained support does not establish

    Distinguish current progress from chapter-start records i

    Each of the four protagonists has chapter-start records and a suspended save for progress within a chapter. At a perch, you can return to the chamber where the four stories are selected, called 方尖塔の間 in the Japanese source. Switching to another protagonist there does not itself discard the previous route’s suspended progress.

    ActionEffect on your progress
    Play another protagonist’s storyRoutes retain separate suspended saves, allowing you to alternate between them.
    Restart the current or an earlier chapter in the same routeThe route’s suspended progress is lost. You resume from the selected chapter’s starting state.
    Clear a replayed chapter and advanceReplay results carry into subsequent chapters and can overwrite their existing starting records. This does not merge new results into an untouched later record.

    Choose your purpose and return point before replaying i

    1. If you only want to watch a conversation or movie, first check whether it is available for review in the story-selection chamber. If it is, replaying a chapter is unnecessary.
    2. To return before losing an ally or retry a missed recruitment, item, or event, inspect a chapter-start state from before the missed opportunity. If you lacked preparation time, you may need an earlier chapter. This helps choose where to restart; it does not remove the recruitment requirements.
    3. Review what you would give up in the current route’s suspended progress. If you need to preserve progress made since the chapter began, do not confirm a chapter restart. Playing another route temporarily is a different choice.
    4. Use chapter selection to resume from your chosen starting point. If you only intend to revisit an earlier situation, check that the later chapter-start record you want still exists, and decide whether to return before clearing forward and replacing it with the replay results.

    Example: replaying Chapter 4 from the middle of Chapter 5 i

    Nintendo DREAM WEB illustrates the distinction with Leda’s suspended Chapter 5 progress and her Chapter 4 starting state. Being able to return to Chapter 5 does not mean returning to the exact point where you left it.

    1. After returning to the story-selection chamber during Chapter 5, the suspended Chapter 5 save is distinct from the existing Chapter 5 starting record.
    2. Starting Chapter 4 again removes the suspended progress from Chapter 5. That action alone does not remove the existing Chapter 5 starting record.
    3. Before overwriting it by clearing forward, you can select the surviving Chapter 5 starting record. You return to the start of Chapter 5, without restoring the actions previously completed partway through it. To select that record, return to the story-selection chamber from a perch.
    4. If you clear Chapter 4 again and continue with the new results, subsequent chapter-start data is overwritten. If you want to retain the old Chapter 5 starting state, treat clearing forward as a different decision from merely viewing an earlier event.

    Replay and carryover answer different questions i

    Chapter replay offers an earlier starting point for retrying an ally loss, recruitment deadline, missed item or event, or unwanted training outcome. It is not a way to undo only the last action or duplicate an arbitrary save slot. You may have to return to a chapter’s beginning and replay the intervening progress.

    What carries into another route is separate from which point you can return to within the same route. Carryover guidance about retained elements does not establish that suspended progress can be restored. This guide follows Nintendo DREAM WEB’s explanation; a separate method for recovering the discarded suspended save has not been confirmed.

    Reported support repetition: what the player did iiii

    A community player in Part I reported gaining support through meals, restarting the chapter, and retaining support while chapter progress reverted. A second post also reports retaining support from gifts and bird interaction. The sequence below summarizes those reports; it has not been reproduced across every route, chapter or version.

    Before trying this, decide whether you can give up your progress within the chapter. A report of retained support does not mean the suspended save survives. If you need to preserve the current training, recruitment or story results, do not start the repetition.

    1. Invite the target to an inn meal to build support. If you have never given that person a gift, do so first to enable meal invitations. Someone who is away may be unavailable.
    2. The player advanced to the next week through individual actions and repeated meals. This advances the chapter’s weekly schedule, so distinguish the experiment from a plan to continue the current run unchanged.
    3. The reported method then used the system menu’s chapter-restart option before completing the chapter’s main progression. It is not a method of undoing only the meal by closing the game or performing an ordinary reload.
    4. After restarting, compare support between the same characters and the chapter-start state. Stop repeating if the expected support was not retained; these sources do not establish the cause of differing results or guaranteed reproduction conditions.

    What retained support does not establish iiii

    • Nintendo DREAM WEB separately describes support carryover. This supports distinguishing support from other progress, but does not validate the community meal loop or identical savings on every repetition. These sources also do not establish whether the behavior is intended or a bug.
    • High support does not make every character immediately recruitable. In addition to support with the current protagonist, you still need the relevant renown, items or negotiations, availability and story conditions. Collecting support conversations is also distinct from completing recruitment.
    • The original player describes saving the gold spent on meals. Do not extend this to a guarantee that every material, currency or unrelated reward returns to the desired state. Retained support does not restore discarded suspended progress; clearing forward can also overwrite later chapter-start records.
