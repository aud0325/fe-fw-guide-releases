    Weapon repair and forging

    Choose between repair and forging after the Chapter 6 smithy unlock, preserve durability for upgrades, and gather missing ore.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide
    1. Carry spare weapons before Chapter 6
    2. Forge for strength; repair for durability
    3. Use some durability before forging, but stop short of zero
    4. Missing materials or no further upgrade
    5. If durability has already reached zero

    Carry spare weapons before Chapter 6 ii

    The smithy unlocks in Part I Chapter 6. Until then, repair and forging are unavailable: buy spare weapons from weapon shops or merchants, and reserve durability-consuming combat arts for fights that need them.

    Once unlocked, smithies are available in locations including Dagsion and Megaira.

    Forge for strength; repair for durability i

    Both services consume gold and materials. Forging costs more than repair, so prioritize weapons your main fighters actually use. If you only need durability for more combat arts, repair uses fewer materials.

    ServiceResultWhen to use it
    ForgeImproved performance and the upgraded weapon’s durabilityYou intend to keep using the weapon and can afford its upgrade
    RepairRestore the current weapon’s durabilityMaintenance matters more than an upgrade, or the weapon is already fully forged

    Use some durability before forging, but stop short of zero i

    1. Use some durability through combat arts before the planned upgrade. Forging applies the upgraded weapon’s durability, reducing the need to pay for repair first.
    2. Switch to a spare when durability runs low. Game8 reports that a zero-durability weapon cannot be forged directly.
    3. At the smithy, check the next upgrade’s performance, materials and gold before forging. Once fully upgraded, use repair for later maintenance.

    Missing materials or no further upgrade i

    Ore requirements vary by weapon. Upgrades normally stop at +1, with some reaching +2; some weapons cannot be forged.

    Break dungeon crystals and red-glowing crates or barrels, or explore world-map gathering spots. Dungeon ore respawns on re-entry. Smithing Stone can be found in Noah’s Cave, for example. Drops are not guaranteed; check how much you still need.

    If durability has already reached zero ii

    Zero durability does not mean permanent loss. The weapon suffers reduced might and accuracy, so use a replacement in battle and choose repair at the smithy. Repair restores durability to its maximum.

    If you still intend to upgrade it, check forging eligibility and cost again after repairing. An expensive upgrade is unnecessary when durability recovery is your only goal.
