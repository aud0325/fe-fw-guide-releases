    Part I training guide

    Ranged healing

    1. BeginnerDiviner
    2. →SpecialtyPriest
    3. →AdvancedBishop

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop dedicated ranged healing. Healing +10 and range +1 make Priest and Bishop useful for building magic and resistance. Follow the front line from a safe distance.

    Skill availability Lv.20 Distant Healing · Lv.35 Distant Healing+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Black magic can fill an offensive gap, but white magic makes better use of her healing skills. Add offense when there is time to develop both schools.
