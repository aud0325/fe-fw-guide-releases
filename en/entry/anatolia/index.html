    Training guide

    1. Advanced classOvate
    2. Master classDruid

    Why this route works

    Druid’s 30-point magic and 15-point speed growth bonuses build on already strong magic and speed, with doubled black-magic uses. It also permits brawling weapons, preserving her favored option for close counterattacks.

    Tactics & limitations

    Her arts offer different power and HP-cost tiers, with area attacks also able to destroy obstacles. Repeatedly choosing the strongest option combines HP loss with limited physical durability. Attacking builds her Blaze gauge; consider remaining HP, gauge and the next enemy attack together.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Continue from the joining Ovate class into Druid, where brawling offers a close-range option.
