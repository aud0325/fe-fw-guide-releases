    Part I training guide

    A mobile attacker using starting lance and flying ranks

    1. Beginner classSoldierLance D
    2. IntermediateWing SoldierFemale · Lance C · Flying D; staying in this intermediate class is an option

    Prepare Lance C for Wing Soldier. Check actual ranks when recruited in another class; choose the sword alternative when sword offense matters more than flight.

    Ranks are promotion preparation targets. Check the recruited class, equipment and ranks, and skip earlier stages. There is no need to restart. Check renown, permits and unlocks on the class page.

    Why this route works

    The listed Wooden Lance, Lance D and Flying D and the female condition support preparing lance Wing Soldier. Hit +20 from an attack-speed advantage is not sword-specific, so the starting lance can be used first.

    Tactics & limitations

    Weapon weight can prevent the attack-speed condition even with good personal speed. Check the actual combat forecast.

    If you want to adjust growth or change roles

    Swords add training from the listed E+ toward D, C and B. Check weight and actual attack speed to maintain the personal ability condition while continuing through Myrmidon and Shido.

    1. Beginner classGladiatorSword D
    2. IntermediateMyrmidonSword C
    3. Advanced classShidoSword B
    Directions for Parts II and III

    In Part II, continue the main weapon, magic and role chosen in Part I. These master and divine classes are options after their Part III unlocks, not a sequence you must complete.

    Flight can continue into Celestial Trooper with the female condition, Flying C and Lance A. A sword switch leads toward Shido and Swordmaster; prepare a Sword S divine route only for that choice.

    Celestial Trooper can continue the flying role. Consider the party’s need for flight before switching to a divine class.

    • Master classCelestial Trooper

      Female · Flying C · Sword A or Lance A

      Unlocked after completing Part III Salvation segment 1

    The listed ranks improve exam chances; they do not guarantee passing. Prepare the divine class’s S-rank target, any additional skill rank and its class-change item together.

    Recommendation basis and sources

    The guide uses growth rates, personal abilities, skill strengths, class conditions and documented starting equipment and ranks. A baseline profile does not guarantee the same state at every recruitment time. Routes and roles are our own AI recommendations; original links are in Sources.
