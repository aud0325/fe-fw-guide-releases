    Training guide

    1. Advanced classGuardian

    Why this route works

    Remaining Guardian reflects a class lock, not a freely chosen optimal promotion path. Spears, white magic and +10 magic healing support a combined physical-wall and healing role within that restriction.

    Tactics & limitations

    After combat, a defense-based chance adds 1 defense up to +10, lasting only until the map ends. This is neither permanent growth nor a guaranteed trigger each fight. Low speed and resistance remain weak against fast or magical enemies, with late joining leaving little training time.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Cannot change class through certification. Use Guardian’s spears and white magic for defense and healing.

    Imported guide and source

    This guide summarizes and translates a recommended route brought over from Game8’s Japanese character guide. Training choices can vary with party composition and your goals. Check skill and unlock conditions on each class page.
