    Training guide

    Guardian lances and healing

    1. AdvancedGuardian

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Use lances and white magic in his fixed Guardian class. High defense growth and post-combat defense stacks suit physical front-line duty. Share enemies while staying near allies who need healing.

    Role choices and adjustments

    Prepare lance gear and useful healing spells rather than changing class. Pair him with an ally who can handle magic enemies.
