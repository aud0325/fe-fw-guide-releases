    Part I training guide

    Archery against afflicted targets

    1. BeginnerHunter
    2. →SpecialtyArcher
    3. →AdvancedSniper

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop fast archery that finishes afflicted enemies. High speed and dexterity growth suit Archer and Sniper, while Tyranny adds attack and critical potential. Coordinate with allies applying status effects.

    Skill availability Lv.20 Menace · Lv.35 Menace+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Switch to Forest Knight when firing positions require mobility. Sniper offers more dexterity growth, so weigh the map’s movement needs.
