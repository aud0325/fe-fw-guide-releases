    First gifts and scouting

    Check recruitment in your current route, introduce yourself with a gift, then prepare support through weekly gifts and meals. Diagnose absence, event timing and negotiations when meeting the renown and support requirements is not enough.

    In this guide
    1. Start with your route and the role you need
    2. Use the first gift to open meals and scouting
    3. Allocate weekly gifts and meals to your targets
    4. Prepare renown, requests and events before recruiting
    5. When recruitment fails, diagnose it before grinding more

    Start with your route and the role you need ii

    Normal scouting recruits eligible characters during free time. The same character can require different renown, support and additional conditions depending on whether you play as Cai, Dietrich, Theodora or Leda. Check eligibility in your current route before buying gifts or negotiation items based on another route’s lower requirements.

    Open the character list from the base map to inspect availability and requirements. The support requirement concerns the relationship between your current protagonist and the target character; support between that character and a different ally does not satisfy it.

    Identify a missing role, such as a frontline fighter, healer or bow user, then choose a candidate whose requirements you can meet. Recruiting a character you intend to train as soon as their conditions allow gives you more opportunities to use and develop them.

    Treat tutorial recruits separately from ordinary scouting candidates. For characters marked as automatic recruits on this site, follow the story or recruitment tutorial in that route. Supporting records may retain another guide’s support and renown figures; those figures do not turn an automatic recruit into an ordinary scouting target.

    Use the first gift to open meals and scouting ii

    “Any gift works” describes the condition for opening the initial interaction. It does not mean every gift raises support by the same amount. Choose liked gifts when your goal is to build support efficiently.

    1. Give the target character one gift first. Nintendo DREAM WEB explains that any gift works for this initial introduction, and that a character who has never received a gift cannot be invited to a meal or scouted.
    2. After the first gift, check for that character when selecting dining companions at the inn. Opening meal invitations is separate from meeting the full recruitment requirements, so continue checking support, renown and any extra conditions.
    3. If you have already given the first gift but cannot invite the character, check whether they are absent. Characters away from Dagsion may be unavailable for meals. Do not assume the gift failed and buy more unnecessarily; check again when they return.

    Allocate weekly gifts and meals to your targets ii

    Before a character joins, you cannot prepare their support by fighting or training together as allies. Gifts and meals are the basic tools. Game8 lists one gift per scouting target each week, so plan regular interactions with your chosen candidates rather than trying to meet the requirement through repeated gifts in a single day.

    Weekly inn meals let you select up to three characters. Prioritize recruitment targets and check their reactions when choosing a dish: food preferences affect support gains. The diners also recover HP and spell uses. Meals cost half as much on Sunday, but if absence or an approaching recruitment opportunity matters, weigh that opportunity against waiting for the discount.

    Gift shops have limited stock and purchases cost gold. Nintendo DREAM WEB points to the tavern in lower Dagsion for relatively inexpensive gifts. The same guide identifies Saraminian Sweets in Megaira as a relatively inexpensive, unlimited-stock option for introductions and birthday gifts; check that you can reach the city in your current progression. Start with introduction gifts and liked items for this week’s targets to avoid spreading your money across characters you cannot yet recruit.

    Prepare renown, requests and events before recruiting iiii

    1. Check renown and support separately in the target’s requirements for your route. Enough support from gifts does not make up for missing renown. Build renown through story progress, completed requests and temple audiences; for requests, check the objectives and deadline and finish them rather than only accepting them.
    2. If negotiation or a requested item is listed, check the gold amount, item type, quantity and any additional actions. Meeting support and renown alone may not finish recruitment in that conversation. Start time-consuming negotiations as soon as they become available.
    3. For a character who requires a paralogue, check when it appears and its acceptance window in your route. Completing it may still leave a chapter, renown, support or negotiation requirement. Unlocking scouting is not the same as automatic recruitment.
    4. Once ready, speak to the character during free time and attempt scouting. Resolve any additional request before returning. If they refuse, use the checks below to identify the missing condition.

    When recruitment fails, diagnose it before grinding more iii

    ProblemWhat to check and do next
    Cannot invite them to the first mealCheck whether you have given them a gift at least once. If so, check whether they are away from Dagsion and wait for another opportunity to meet.
    Not scoutable in this routeMore gifts and renown do not remove a route restriction. Confirm you have not used another route’s requirements. Being unavailable for normal Part I scouting also does not necessarily mean they never join anywhere in the game.
    Missing renown or supportMeet both the current protagonist’s support with the target and the renown required in this route. Combine weekly gifts and meals with story progress, requests and audiences.
    Stats are sufficient, but scouting is unavailable nowCheck appearance chapter, paralogue, event progress and negotiation requirements. If a known timing condition remains, progress the relevant story or event and speak again. Do not assume passing days is sufficient; check any limited-time paralogues or requests first.
    Refused during negotiationCheck item type and quantity, gold, dialogue choices and whether another visit is required. A single refusal does not necessarily mean recruitment is impossible.
