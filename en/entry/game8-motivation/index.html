    Restore motivation before training

    Check the motivation of the companions you plan to train, then choose meals, gifts, theatre, baths or lodging by party needs and remaining uses. Compare costs, unlocks and turn consumption.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide
    1. Who needs motivation first?
    2. Recovery methods, costs and when to use them
    3. A practical recovery sequence before training
    4. Theatre and bath conditions to watch
    5. Saving both gold and motivation

    Who needs motivation first? ii

    Motivation is spent mainly on arena training and temple service. Training raises skills used to learn combat arts and abilities, equip weapons and prepare for higher-class requirements. Temple service earns divine blessings and is limited to once a week. Start with the companions you intend to train or take to service this week, rather than restoring everyone indiscriminately.

    After clearing Part I Chapter 4, speak to the receptionist outside the Grand Arena for training. Registration is open from 06:00 to 23:59, with two participants per session. Each of the four training types is available once a week; restoring motivation does not restore an already-used training opportunity. Check each participant’s current motivation, target skills, unused training types and your gold before choosing a recovery method.

    Recovery methods, costs and when to use them iiii

    Meals, gifts, theatre visits and baths restore motivation without spending a turn. Lodging fully restores everyone but advances time by one turn. Some weekly limits depend on facility unlocks or upgrades, so check your remaining uses before committing.

    Method and costParticipants and limitsEffects and best use
    Meal at the inn · 300GUp to 3 characters · 2–3 times weeklyRestores motivation and raises support. A useful first choice for several trainees, but disliked dishes do not restore motivation.
    Gift · consumes a gift you ownOnce per character per weekTops up one character’s motivation and support. Useful when one companion still needs recovery after a meal, provided you have a gift available.
    Watch a play · 500G2 characters · once weekly · unlocks at renown 7Raises support and restores motivation, with recovery varying by play preference. Use it for extra recovery after exhausting meal opportunities.
    Bath · 500G2 characters · unlocks at renown 8 · see limits belowRestores 1 motivation segment and all HP and spell uses, while providing support and temporary abilities. Useful for upcoming combatants after exploration.
    Lodging · 400G + 1 turnAll companionsFully restores everyone’s motivation, HP and spell uses. Consider it when many companions are depleted, after checking how much free time remains.

    A practical recovery sequence before training iii

    1. Choose the two trainees first and check their motivation individually. If another companion also needs support, decide whether to include them in the meal.
    2. A companion must have received an introductory gift before you can invite them to a meal. At the inn, choose a meal and inspect each character’s reaction to the dish. Avoid disliked food and restore up to three companions together. Afterwards, check whether your intended trainees still need motivation.
    3. If only one character needs more recovery, check whether they have received a gift this week and whether you have one to give. If meals are exhausted and two characters need help, check theatre or bath availability and remaining uses. Baths are especially useful if HP or spell uses are also depleted.
    4. If the whole party is depleted and individual recovery is impractical, consider lodging for 400G. Its gold price may look attractive, but it costs a turn. Check that you can still finish remaining weekly activities and time-sensitive tasks before staying.
    5. After restoring motivation, keep enough gold for training and return to the arena receptionist. Low motivation, insufficient gold, closed registration and a used weekly training slot are separate problems; check each if you cannot train.

    Theatre and bath conditions to watch iiii

    To watch a play, visit the theatre receptionist in Dagsion’s entertainment district. The motivation guide places access around Part I Chapter 9, while the dedicated theatre guide specifies renown 7 as the unlock. Check your renown and facility availability rather than relying on a chapter number. Preferred plays are beneficial. Performing is a separate activity: performing that week prevents watching, so do not choose it first if you need motivation recovery.

    Baths unlock at renown 8. The motivation guide’s three weekly uses should not be treated as a fixed limit after upgrades. The dedicated bath guide says renown 9 expands access to four uses Monday–Saturday and two on Sunday, six in total. Its recovery section also mentions up to five recoveries, so the total recovery count is inconsistent within that article. Plan around the remaining uses displayed in your game and distinguish this from the stated recovery of one motivation segment per bath.

    Bath abilities last through the current chapter’s main battle. Choose companions you plan to field and check the calendar for that week’s baths. Special baths are available on Sunday, but you need not delay necessary training recovery just to wait for them. The sources checked here do not specify exact motivation segments for meals, gifts or theatre; do not assume they match baths or always restore motivation fully.

    Saving both gold and motivation ii

    Meals are half price on Sunday, and training is half price on Saturday and Sunday. Use discount days when deadlines and remaining activities allow, but do not spend your training budget on recovery. If your aim is skill EXP, also check arena spectating: it awards party skill EXP without costing gold or motivation. Its event windows are short, so check the receptionist or calendar.
