    Part I training guide

    Keeping Charioteer for the personal ability

    1. IntermediateCharioteerKeep the recruited class; starts with Bow C and Riding C

    Start in the recruited Charioteer class. There is no need to return to Hunter or optimize a particular mount.

    Ranks are promotion preparation targets. Check the recruited class, equipment and ranks, and skip earlier stages. There is no need to restart. Check renown, permits and unlocks on the class page.

    Why this route works

    Level 20 Charioteer, Iron Bow, Bow C and Riding C are listed at recruitment, so continue that class. Personal Hit +20 applies only as Charioteer; promotion to an advanced class is not mandatory.

    Tactics & limitations

    Changing to a higher-tier class changes the personal bonus’s applicability. This recommendation uses accuracy and preparation, not mount-growth optimization.

    If you want to adjust growth or change roles

    Switch to Forest Knight if its combat and movement role suits the party better, while accounting for the loss of the Charioteer-specific accuracy bonus.

    1. Beginner classHunterBow D
    2. IntermediateArcherBow C
    3. Advanced classForest KnightBow B · Riding D
    Directions for Parts II and III

    In Part II, continue the main weapon, magic and role chosen in Part I. These master and divine classes are options after their Part III unlocks, not a sequence you must complete.

    Keeping Charioteer remains an option. Other bow or divine classes lose the Charioteer-only personal bonus, so compare accuracy, movement and the attack role.

    • Master classBow Adept

      Bow A

      Unlocked after completing Part III Salvation segment 1

    • Divine classThe Calamity

      Bow S (Sword S also qualifies)

      From Part III Salvation segment 4. Obtain the class-change item at the Crown Temple; the Crown Key is also needed. · Class-change item: Dark Crystal

    The listed ranks improve exam chances; they do not guarantee passing. Prepare the divine class’s S-rank target, any additional skill rank and its class-change item together.

    Recommendation basis and sources

    The guide uses growth rates, personal abilities, skill strengths, class conditions and documented starting equipment and ranks. A baseline profile does not guarantee the same state at every recruitment time. Routes and roles are our own AI recommendations; original links are in Sources.
