    Training guide

    1. Advanced classBardinger
    2. Master classOrichalda
    3. Divine classThe Cavalier

    Why this route works

    Sword and Riding proficiencies lead into Orichalda’s movement 7 and three additional combat-art slots. The Cavalier provides movement 8 and immunity to cavalry-effective damage, improving mobility while removing that specific mounted weakness.

    Tactics & limitations

    When defending, +3 attack speed and +10 evasion support enemy-phase counters. They do not guarantee safety with low resistance and moderate HP/defense. A Part-I quest feeds into Part-II recruitment, so check that prerequisite before planning the build.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Train Riding in Bardinger and use his Sword proficiency toward Orichalda and The Cavalier.

    Imported guide and source

    This guide summarizes and translates a recommended route brought over from Game8’s Japanese character guide. Training choices can vary with party composition and your goals. Check skill and unlock conditions on each class page.
