    Part I training guide

    A sword initiator using attack-speed support

    1. Beginner classGladiatorSword D
    2. IntermediateMyrmidonSword C
    3. Advanced classShidoSword B

    Ranks are promotion preparation targets. Check the recruited class, equipment and ranks, and skip earlier stages. There is no need to restart. Check renown, permits and unlocks on the class page.

    Why this route works

    The listed Bronze Sword, Sword D and Infantry D, high speed and initiating Attack Speed +3 fit continued swords. An optional Brigand detour can be prepared with Sword C when strength needs help.

    Tactics & limitations

    The attack-speed bonus requires initiating combat. Check enemy-phase speed, resistance and weapon weight separately.

    If you want to adjust growth or change roles

    Use Brigand only for the intermediate stage when strength needs help. Sword C qualifies, so the axe weakness need not create extra training.

    1. Beginner classGladiatorSword D
    2. IntermediateBrigandSword C or Axe C
    3. Advanced classShidoSword B

    Brigand+15+5
    Directions for Parts II and III

    In Part II, continue the main weapon, magic and role chosen in Part I. These master and divine classes are options after their Part III unlocks, not a sequence you must complete.

    • Master classSwordmaster

      Sword A

      Complete Mars’s Request after restoring Mars Shrine in Part III Salvation segment 3.

    • Divine classThe Blade

      Sword S

      From Part III Salvation segment 4. Obtain the class-change item at the Crown Temple; the Crown Key is also needed. · Class-change item: Demonic Blade Guard

    The listed ranks improve exam chances; they do not guarantee passing. Prepare the divine class’s S-rank target, any additional skill rank and its class-change item together.

    Recommendation basis and sources

    The guide uses growth rates, personal abilities, skill strengths, class conditions and documented starting equipment and ranks. A baseline profile does not guarantee the same state at every recruitment time. Routes and roles are our own AI recommendations; original links are in Sources.

    An AI-generated beginner guide based on growth rates, personal abilities and skill strengths. Training choices can vary with party composition and your goals. Mount-growth optimization and fusion are outside its scope.
