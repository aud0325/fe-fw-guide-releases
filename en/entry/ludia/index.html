    Part I training guide

    Sword follow-ups and evasion

    1. BeginnerGladiator
    2. →SpecialtyMyrmidon
    3. →AdvancedShido

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop sword follow-ups using high speed and the initiating attack-speed bonus. Myrmidon and Shido help activate Insight’s evasion too. A lighter sword can expand the range of enemies she can follow up against.

    Skill availability Lv.20 Perception · Lv.35 Perception+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Use Brigand for the intermediate stage when damage is low. Strength growth rises from 35% to 50% and speed falls from 70% to 60%, fitting a fast unit who needs more power.
