    Part I training guide

    Back-line magic and support

    1. Beginner classDivinerBlack Magic D
    2. IntermediateShamanBlack Magic C
    3. Advanced classOvateBlack Magic B

    Ranks are promotion preparation targets. Check the recruited class, equipment and ranks, and skip earlier stages. There is no need to restart. Check renown, permits and unlocks on the class page.

    Why this route works

    Fire is listed among her starting spells, and magic growth and both magic strengths support a caster. Choose Shaman and Ovate for offense, or check available healing spells before taking Priest and Bishop for healing.

    Tactics & limitations

    The personal ability supports allied combat-art range; it does not extend every spell. Low HP and defense make protection important.

    If you want to adjust growth or change roles

    A bishop path gives a clear healing and support role. Even when using both types of magic, prioritize the rank needed for the main class.

    1. Beginner classDivinerWhite Magic D
    2. IntermediatePriestWhite Magic C
    3. Advanced classBishopWhite Magic B
    Directions for Parts II and III

    In Part II, continue the main weapon, magic and role chosen in Part I. These master and divine classes are options after their Part III unlocks, not a sequence you must complete.

    Druid continues offense; Wiseman continues healing. Mobile hybrid Valkyrium is a separate option requiring riding preparation and its later unlock.

    • Master classDruid

      Black Magic A

      Unlocked after completing Part III Salvation segment 1

    • Divine classThe Avatar

      Black Magic S (White Magic S also qualifies)

      From Part III Salvation segment 4. Obtain the class-change item at the Crown Temple; the Crown Key is also needed. · Class-change item: Lotus of Rebirth

    The listed ranks improve exam chances; they do not guarantee passing. Prepare the divine class’s S-rank target, any additional skill rank and its class-change item together.

    Recommendation basis and sources

    The guide uses growth rates, personal abilities, skill strengths, class conditions and documented starting equipment and ranks. A baseline profile does not guarantee the same state at every recruitment time. Routes and roles are our own AI recommendations; original links are in Sources.
