    Discovering Heretics’ Cave

    Reveal the cave at the pass northwest of Dagsion, then collect its chest items and smithing materials.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide
    1. If the cave is missing: explore the pass first
    2. What to collect from the chests
    3. Gathering ore in Part I and deciding when to visit
    4. Material changes in Part III · Spoilers

    If the cave is missing: explore the pass first i

    Heretics’ Cave is in the northern part of the world map, near the southeast of Thaleia. To reveal it from Dagsion, first find and explore the pass northwest of the capital.

    1. Travel northwest from Dagsion to Wodan Pass (editorial romanization). Its official localized name has not been confirmed.
    2. Explore at the pass. This exploration is the action that reveals the destination; do not rely on simply travelling past it.
    3. Find the newly revealed cave north of the pass and head there. Keep the two directions distinct: northwest from Dagsion, then north from the pass.

    What to collect from the chests ii

    The cave guide lists a Vulnerary and Mist of Protection (editorial translation) as chest rewards. Check the treasure chests even if you came for ore; these rewards are separate from the random materials found by breaking crates and barrels.

    ItemUse and identification
    VulneraryRestores 20 HP to its user. Keep it as a healing consumable for later combat.
    Mist of Protection (editorial translation)Listed by the cave guide as an item useful in battle. Its localized name and exact effect are not confirmed here; read the in-game description before deciding whom to use it on.

    Gathering ore in Part I and deciding when to visit ii

    Game8’s Part I dungeon table lists Smithing Stone, Jade Steel, Thunderstone and Magic Array here. Jade Steel and Magic Array are editorial translations; their official English equivalents have not been confirmed.

    Ore can drop when you break crates and barrels inside dungeons. The ore guide reports better drop chances from glowing containers, so check those first when gathering materials. The list does not mean all four materials are guaranteed on every visit.

    Ore is used to repair and forge weapons at the smithy, and the required material varies by weapon. Check the requirements of the weapon you want to maintain or improve before setting out, so you know which material and quantity you are looking for.

    The cited guide does not specify the cave’s first accessible chapter, recommended level, enemy layout or internal junctions. This page covers discovery and item collection; it does not establish a chapter at which the cave is guaranteed safe to clear.

    Material changes in Part III · Spoilers
    i

    The Part III Salvation arc table lists Heretics’ Cave again. Its materials are Smithing Stone, Jade Steel, Thunderstone, Magic Array and the additional Battle Soul Stone (editorial translations where English equivalents remain unconfirmed). Reassess the cave against your later material needs even if you visited in Part I. This describes the ore list, not a claim that previously opened treasure chests refill.
