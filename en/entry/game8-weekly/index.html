    Weekly activities and discounts

    Choose weekly activities for skill training, recruitment, or battle preparation, using weekend discounts and each temple’s favorable day.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide

    Choose facilities around this week’s goal ii

    Weekly activities serve different purposes. Use the arena for skill experience before a class exam, gifts and meals for recruitment support, and recovery, blessings, or bath effects for the next battle. Weekdays below refer to the in-game calendar. A discount is a way to reduce spending, not a reason to postpone needed training or recovery until after a battle.

    Why plan training and meals for the weekend? ii

    Arena training does not immediately change a character’s class. It provides experience in the selected skill; raising that skill level improves the chance of passing a class certification exam. First identify the skill needed for the intended class, then choose allies to train it. This preparation is why Game8 places training first in its weekly recommendations.

    Training costs half as much on Saturday and Sunday, making the weekend economical when no immediate exam or battle requires it sooner. Inn meals are half price on Sunday only, and you can select up to three characters. For recruitment, choose targets whose food preferences match; for existing allies, also consider who is short on HP or spell uses.

    Each of the four arena training types is available once per week, for up to two characters per session. Training costs gold and motivation: check that the type is unused this week and prepare both resources for the participants. Weekend discounts do not restore used sessions.

    Distinguish recruitment support from combat assistance iii

    Give a character their first gift to enable meal invitations. A scoutable character can receive a gift once each week, so select recruitment targets before preparing gifts they like. Preferred gifts give a larger support increase, and meals also vary with food preferences. Increased support alone does not mean immediate recruitment: check that character’s route-specific renown, support, and requested-item requirements as well.

    White-raven bonding increases support between the protagonist and individual allies. Higher support makes protagonist assistance more likely in both normal and quick battles, giving it a role beyond recruitment. Because assistance remains probabilistic, do not expose an ally on the assumption that the protagonist must intervene.

    Choose a temple by both effect and weekday ii

    Weekly service in the imperial capital is much more likely to succeed on the relevant temple’s day. Sunday is favorable for every temple. The table describes the effects associated with each temple’s blessings; one service does not immediately grant every listed effect. Effects are obtained through blessing-level progression, so consider your current blessings and the next battle. A favorable day still does not guarantee success.

    After renown 7–8, choose between recovery and income ii

    Theatre visits unlock at renown 7 and provide motivation recovery and support. Characters have preferences for plays; prioritize those with the yellow mark at their upper right. Performing unlocks at renown 8, raises support, and pays according to the performance evaluation. Since performing prevents a visit, decide whether motivation recovery or income is the more pressing need.

    Baths unlock at renown 8 and combine support with HP, spell-use and motivation recovery, plus a special skill lasting until the current chapter ends. Prioritizing allies you will actually deploy after free time lets you use both recovery and the skill, rather than choosing solely for support. Do not count that skill as a permanent benefit carried into later chapters.

    After choosing your activities, use the related free-time daily and weekly checklist to record completion. This page compares costs, effects, and weekdays; the checklist tracks what you have actually done that day and week.
