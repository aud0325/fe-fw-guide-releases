    Training guide

    1. Advanced classOvate
    2. Master classDruid

    Why this route works

    Starting from Ovate, favored Black Magic leads into Druid. Its 30-point magic and 15-point speed growth bonuses and doubled black-magic uses strengthen the main offense while preserving swords as an alternative against different defenses.

    Tactics & limitations

    Range-0–10 debuffs can halve enemy attack speed or swap defense and resistance. They expire after one battle or one turn, making attack order important. The speed-halving art costs 18 HP. Preventing maximum-HP loss on burst does not remove an art’s direct HP cost.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Continue Black Magic from the joining Ovate class into Druid, retaining swords as another option.

    Imported guide and source

    This guide summarizes and translates a recommended route brought over from Game8’s Japanese character guide. Training choices can vary with party composition and your goals. Check skill and unlock conditions on each class page.
