    Training guide

    Black magic and debuff arts

    1. AdvancedOvate

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Use black magic and debuff arts to set up allied attacks. Good dexterity and luck growth help this role; artillery also gains Hit +30. Coordinate an allied attack after his debuff.

    Skill availability Lv.20 Winds of Victory · Initially known Cannoning Flair · During Overblaze Swashbuckler's Luck+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Prioritize existing spells and frequent debuff arts. Pair him with a healer when using HP-costing arts repeatedly.
