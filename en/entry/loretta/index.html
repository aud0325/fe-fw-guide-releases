    Part I training guide

    Sword offense at high HP

    1. BeginnerGladiator
    2. →SpecialtyMyrmidon
    3. →AdvancedShido

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop sword speed while keeping HP high. Full HP adds attack and at least half HP adds evasion, so pair her with a healer. Myrmidon and Shido reinforce her high speed growth.

    Skill availability Lv.20 Peak Form · Lv.35 Peak Form+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Use Brigand for the intermediate stage when strength is lacking. Lance cavalry can add mobility if there is time for new proficiencies.
