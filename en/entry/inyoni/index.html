    Role & recommended training i

    1. Archer
    2. Forest Knight
    3. Bow Knight

    Why this route works

    Continuing bows from the joining Archer class into Bow Knight adds mobility and a 15-point speed growth bonus to address slowness. This preserves strong strength and the personal +3 strength with bows while improving firing positions and follow-up potential.

    Tactics & limitations

    Riding D needs separate training; Forest Knight remains the class until Bow Knight unlocks. A growth bonus does not guarantee immediate follow-ups after promotion. Joining from Part-I chapter 7 onward favors using strong single shots while checking actual attack speed.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Prepare Riding D from the joining Archer class for Forest Knight. Bow Knight unlocks in Salvation segment 3.

    A route recommended by the guide, not a mandatory promotion sequence. Check each class for skill requirements and unlock conditions.
