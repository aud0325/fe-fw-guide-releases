    Free-time daily and weekly checklist

    Separate daily care, weekly training and social activities, and time-sensitive events when free time begins, then record completed tasks on this device.

    In this guide
    1. Start with the in-game date and upcoming deadlines
    2. What to check daily, weekly, and when an event appears
    3. A practical order for choosing activities
    4. Benefits and limits of combining tasks on the weekend
    5. Manage the page’s checkmarks yourself

    Start with the in-game date and upcoming deadlines i

    Daily and weekly refer to the in-game calendar, not real-world time. At the start of free time, check the weekday, scheduled matches to watch, and the acceptance windows of paralogues you intend to pursue. Plan dated activities first so that waiting for a discount does not displace a deadline.

    The community example completes one week’s activities on Saturday and begins the next set on Sunday, making Sunday a useful weekly checkpoint. It does not establish an exact reset time. The reported 06:00 arena opening is a facility opening time, not evidence that every weekly activity resets then. Check availability at the relevant facility in the game.

    What to check daily, weekly, and when an event appears ii

    WeeklyInn meals, temple service, white-raven bonding, and giftsGifts are available once per character each week. Check the combined task after finishing your chosen recruitment targets, rather than treating one tick as a record for every character.

    A practical order for choosing activities iii

    This is an order for planning your free time, not a fixed walking route. Choose whether your current priority is training, recruitment, or the next battle before trying to use every facility.

    1. Check the calendar and paralogue windows, then check today’s stable care. Keep dated events and daily tasks separate from weekly activities.
    2. Prioritize arena training for allies preparing for a class exam. It provides experience in the selected skill and support with participants, helping prepare the skill required for the intended exam.
    3. For a recruitment target who has never received a gift, give one first to enable meal invitations. Then use preferred gifts and food to raise support between the current protagonist and that target. Meals can include up to three characters and restore HP and spell uses, so consider both your support target and who needs recovery.
    4. Check temple service and white-raven bonding with the next battle in mind. Service helps obtain blessings; raven bonding raises support that improves the chance of the protagonist assisting. If baths are unlocked, prioritize allies you will field so they can use the recovery and the special skill lasting until the chapter ends.
    5. Mark only completed tasks in the checklist below, then plan outside exploration. Allow for both the time remaining and the return trip, rather than delaying a dated paralogue or match simply to obtain a weekly discount.

    Benefits and limits of combining tasks on the weekend i

    Game8 lists half-price arena training on Saturday and Sunday, and half-price inn meals on Sunday. Each temple has a favorable weekday, while Sunday applies to every temple. With no urgent deadline, combining training, meals, and service on Sunday takes advantage of both discounts and the favorable day.

    A favorable service day improves the success rate; it does not guarantee the highest evaluation or a particular blessing. Waiting for a discount can help when money is tight, but weigh that against an immediate need for skill training or recovery before battle. This checklist is not a requirement to complete every activity.

    Manage the page’s checkmarks yourself

    Checkmarks below are stored in this browser on this device. They do not read your game progress or date and do not sync with other browsers or devices. When an in-game day passes, clear the daily item yourself; when a new week begins, review the weekly items too.

    The reset button clears daily, weekly, and occasional checkmarks together. To revisit only some tasks, uncheck just those items. A checked occasional task still needs another look when new dialogue or a new paralogue appears. Clearing browser storage can also remove this record.

    My checklist

    Saved on this device. It does not sync with game dates; reset manually for a new cycle.
