    Dietrich’s challenge letters

    Find and complete Dietrich’s one-on-one challenges, compare rewards at renown levels 2–10, and understand the combat arts’ uses and drawbacks before Part I ends.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide
    1. Unlock in Chapter 5, then accept further challenges
    2. From accepting a letter to collecting the reward
    3. Destinations and rewards by renown
    4. Compare combat-art conditions before raw power

    Unlock in Chapter 5, then accept further challenges i

    Challenges unlock during Dietrich’s Part I Chapter 5. The first is part of the story; accept subsequent challenges yourself from the broker near the statue in Dagsion’s city district. This is a Dietrich-route activity, not a shared unlock for every protagonist.

    The broker’s offers update as the story progresses, and each has a renown requirement. If raising renown does not reveal an offer, check again after advancing the story. All challenges expire at the end of Part I: you can return after training, but review unfinished challenges before ending the part.

    From accepting a letter to collecting the reward i

    Dietrich fights the challenger alone and cannot receive assistance from companions. Prepare healing that he can use himself before setting out; a normal party plan that depends on another character’s healing will not work here.

    1. Accept the desired challenge from the broker near Dagsion’s statue and check its destination.
    2. Leave for the world map and travel to the designated dungeon marked by a sword icon. The table below pairs each destination with its renown requirement.
    3. Inside the dungeon, use the direction bar at the top of the screen to locate the challenger.
    4. Speak to the challenger to begin a one-on-one fight with Dietrich. Finish preparing your healing before initiating the conversation.
    5. After winning, return to the broker in Dagsion and report completion. Winning in the dungeon does not replace this final step: reporting awards the gold and renown.

    Compare combat-art conditions before raw power i

    Completing challenges early gives you more of the story in which to use their arts, while renown benefits training and meals. However, an art is not always an upgrade over a normal attack. The comparisons below interpret the reported effects; they are neither guaranteed duel strategies nor a claim that every weapon can use every art.

    Art / durability costUseful situations and limitations
    Blood Fang / 3Restores HP equal to half the damage dealt. Useful for attacking while recovering, but its −20 hit modifier makes it an unreliable substitute for all other healing.
    Swallow Fall / 3On hitting a flying foe, reduces its movement by 3 until your next phase. Use it to restrict a flier; do not expect the same movement penalty against other foes.
    Flowing Water / 6Grants +20 avoid and a follow-up regardless of attack speed, but halves damage dealt during combat. Compare the forecast instead of assuming that the extra strike necessarily increases damage.
    Giant Splitter / 5Halves damage received when fighting a large unit. Useful for enduring a large foe’s retaliation, but it is not universal damage reduction.
    Unmoving Piercing Lance / 5Requires a lance with range 1–2 and attacks at range 2. Adds 7 might and grants +20 hit if the foe’s attack speed exceeds yours by at least 4. Check both the weapon range and speed gap.
    Critical Breaker / 4Grants +20 critical avoid during combat and afterward until your next phase. It reduces critical-hit risk; it is not +20 avoid against ordinary attacks.
    Distant Thunder / 30Reaches range 3–5, but costs 30 durability, has −20 hit and cannot follow up. Use its reach deliberately and check remaining durability first.
    Evil-Breaking Iron Fist / 5On hit, disables the foe’s “Blessing of the Underworld,” which returns after one combat. Plan the relevant combat order; the removal is not permanent.
    Absolute Blade / 3Adds 10 might and 100 critical, but sets hit rate to 10 and cannot follow up. The large critical bonus does not make it a dependable finishing attack.
