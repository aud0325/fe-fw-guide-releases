    Why this route works

    Battlemaster adds 25 percentage points each to HP and strength growth while avoiding further speed penalties from armored classes. Elephant Rider is a defensive alternative with damage halving, but it cannot equip weapons.

    Tactics & limitations

    The personal +3 strength with an axe helps damage, not very low speed or resistance. Fast enemies can hit twice and magic can deal heavy damage. Choosing Elephant Rider also means giving up evasion for a damage-taking role.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Game8 prefers axes into Battlemaster over further speed penalties from armor. Elephant Rider is a defensive alternative but cannot equip weapons.

    A route recommended by the guide, not a mandatory promotion sequence. Check each class for skill requirements and unlock conditions.
