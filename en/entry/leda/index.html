    Part I training guide

    An extra-action supporter prepared with bow or sword

    1. Beginner classHunterBow D (Sword D also qualifies)
    2. IntermediateArcherBow C
    3. Advanced classDancerBow B or Sword B · route unlock required

    Choose extra-action support if other attackers are developed, or the Sniper alternative when another attacker is needed.

    Dancer: complete the Dancer’s Successor quest in Leda route Chapter 9. Aim for Sword B or Bow B.

    Ranks are promotion preparation targets. Check the recruited class, equipment and ranks, and skip earlier stages. There is no need to restart. Check renown, permits and unlocks on the class page.

    Why this route works

    Starting sword and bow equipment and both strengths support offense before the unlock. Once unlocked, Dancer offers extra actions while using her high speed.

    Tactics & limitations

    The initiating no-counter ability has only a 5% chance. It should not justify an unsafe attack.

    If you want to adjust growth or change roles

    For continued offense, keep bow training through Archer and Sniper. An existing sword build can prepare Dancer’s Sword B target in sword classes instead.

    1. Beginner classHunterBow D
    2. IntermediateArcherBow C
    3. Advanced classSniperBow B
    Directions for Parts II and III

    In Part II, continue the main weapon, magic and role chosen in Part I. These master and divine classes are options after their Part III unlocks, not a sequence you must complete.

    For continued extra actions, prepare Sword A and Bow C for The Apsara. Shadow Seeker is an option for sword and bow training, not a mandatory prerequisite. Bow Adept is a choice for continued bow offense.

    • Master classShadow Seeker

      Sword A or Bow A · For continuing sword and bow training

      Unlocked after completing Part III Salvation segment 1

    • Divine classThe Apsara

      Sword A · Bow C · No S-rank requirement

      From Part III Salvation segment 4. Obtain the class-change item at the Crown Temple; the Crown Key is also needed. · Class-change item: Muse’s Necklace

      Apsara is an exception with Sword A and Bow C, without an S-rank target. Consider it for a dancer support role, rather than the stronger bow-offense direction of Calamity.

    The listed ranks improve exam chances; they do not guarantee passing. Prepare the divine class’s S-rank target, any additional skill rank and its class-change item together.

    Recommendation basis and sources

    The guide uses growth rates, personal abilities, skill strengths, class conditions and documented starting equipment and ranks. A baseline profile does not guarantee the same state at every recruitment time. Routes and roles are our own AI recommendations; original links are in Sources.
