    Origin

    Mahadu's Right Arm

    Str +6

    Theodora’s right arm holds immense strength: the power once wielded by the hero Mahadu.

    Part I training guide

    Lance healing / flying offense

    1. BeginnerSoldier
    2. →SpecialtyArmored Knight
    3. →AdvancedGuardian/Dragoon

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop lance offense with arts and healing as options. High HP and strength growth support front-line combat and art HP costs. Guardian develops defense and resistance; Dragoon adds flight and access to attack positions.

    Skill availability Lv.5 Direct Hit · Initially known Unstoppable Force · During Overblaze Royal Resolve+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Choose Guardian to share healing duties, or Dragoon for flying access to art positions. In the flying role, let another ally cover healing.
