    Part I training guide

    A lance and healing front-liner with long-range arts

    1. Beginner classSoldierLance D
    2. IntermediateArmored KnightLance C · Armor E+
    3. Advanced classGuardianLance B · Route unlock required

    Use this default when Guardian is unlocked or planned. For a focus on long-range Blaze Arts and mobility, check the Dragoon alternative and its requirements below.

    Guardian: complete Seeking a Challenger: Il-Ila of the Flying Lance at Dietrich route Renown 6, or all Theodora war preparations through Chapter 9. Check the precise start/end deadline in-game for Theodora. Lance B and healing-spell preparation are separate.

    Ranks are promotion preparation targets. Check the recruited class, equipment and ranks, and skip earlier stages. There is no need to restart. Check renown, permits and unlocks on the class page.

    Why this route works

    Guardian remains the default for combining lance and white-magic strengths with high HP and strength. Alongside frontline attacks and healing, strength-based throwing Blaze Arts can reach distant enemies. The Dragoon alternative below suits long-range attacks and flexible positioning.

    Tactics & limitations

    Throwing Blaze Arts consume HP, so plan healing and survival after repeated use. Bursting also reduces maximum HP. The damage-halving ability activates only 30% of the time; do not rely on it when ending a ranged attack within reach of several enemies.

    If you want to adjust growth or change roles

    Dragoon uses flight to find firing positions for her throwing Blaze Arts. Keep the Lance B investment and add Flying C. Flight improves positioning rather than increasing the arts’ own range. Let allies cover Guardian’s healing role; if Dragoon’s unlock was missed, Light Cavalry and Bardinger remain lance cavalry alternatives.

    1. Beginner classSoldierLance D
    2. IntermediateArmored KnightLance C · Armor E+; may prepare in the current class
    3. Advanced classDragoonFlying C · Lance B · Route unlock required

    Dragoon: complete Aurora’s request at Cai route Renown 8, or all Theodora war preparations through Chapter 11. Prepare Flying C plus Lance B or Axe B.

    Directions for Parts II and III

    In Part II, continue the main weapon, magic and role chosen in Part I. These master and divine classes are options after their Part III unlocks, not a sequence you must complete.

    Guardian prepares Lance A for Sentinel and Lance S if choosing The Trident. A Dragoon can keep combining flight with long-range arts later on. Choose between frontline healing and mobile ranged attacks before giving up flight for a higher tier.

    • Master classSentinel

      Lance A · Healing-spell ranks separate

      Unlocked after completing Part III Salvation segment 1

    • Divine classThe Trident

      Lance S

      From Part III Salvation segment 4. Obtain the class-change item at the Crown Temple; the Crown Key is also needed. · Class-change item: Three-Pronged Spear

    The listed ranks improve exam chances; they do not guarantee passing. Prepare the divine class’s S-rank target, any additional skill rank and its class-change item together.

    Recommendation basis and sources

    The guide uses growth rates, personal abilities, skill strengths, class conditions and documented starting equipment and ranks. A baseline profile does not guarantee the same state at every recruitment time. Routes and roles are our own AI recommendations; original links are in Sources.
