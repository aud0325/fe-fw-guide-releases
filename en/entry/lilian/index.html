    Part I training guide

    Accurate archery

    1. BeginnerHunter
    2. →SpecialtyArcher
    3. →AdvancedSniper

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop accurate archery from beyond counter range. This combines Safety First with Feeling Good, while Archer and Sniper reinforce her high dexterity growth. Favor a more accurate weapon when needed to reach 100% hit.

    Skill availability Lv.20 Upper Hand · Lv.35 Upper Hand+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Forest Knight adds mobility. Sniper offers more dexterity growth, so compare movement with equipment that preserves 100% hit.

    Keep the chosen advanced class in Part II. These master and divine classes are role-based options from Part III, not a sequence to complete.
