    Part I training guide

    Fast sword offense

    1. BeginnerGladiator
    2. →SpecialtyMyrmidon
    3. →AdvancedShido

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop fast sword offense. Myrmidon and Shido build speed and dexterity for follow-ups and evasion. Use Swap to arrange allied positions, then select a safe target.

    Skill availability Lv.20 Flickering Edge · Lv.35 Flickering Edge+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Use Brigand for the intermediate stage when strength is lacking. Bows offer range when the party needs it, with a new main proficiency to train.
