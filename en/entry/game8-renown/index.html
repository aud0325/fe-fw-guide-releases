    Renown by route

    Reach renown 4 for intermediate certification, then use route activities, temple audiences and timed requests to work toward recruitment and facility unlocks. Renown, support and exam success are separate requirements.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide
    1. Start with renown 4, then target the recruits you need
    2. Use the activities available to your current protagonist
    3. Shared sources to check during free time
    4. What higher renown actually enables
    5. When higher renown still does not solve the problem

    Start with renown 4, then target the recruits you need ii

    Renown is a protagonist-specific level raised by points from story progress, completed requests and other activities. The upper-right display shows your current level and the points needed for the next one. Early on, aim for renown 4 to access intermediate certification; afterward, set your next target around the requirements of recruits in your current route.

    Reaching renown 4 does not automatically promote anyone. Visit the certification examiner in northern Dagsion, select a class and check its seal requirement and displayed success rate. Character level and skill ranks also affect that rate, and a failed attempt still consumes a seal. Train the skills needed for your chosen class while raising renown.

    Use the activities available to your current protagonist iii

    Main-story progress is a basic source of substantial renown gains. During free time, combine it with the activities below, checking timed requests before advancing the chapter. These special activities are not shared by every protagonist.

    ProtagonistWhere to go and what to doWhat to check
    CaiFavors unlock through Chapter 3 story progress. Find hand icons on the map or above people, then speak to the requester to accept.Prioritize the earliest deadlines: an overdue favor fails. Completion awards renown and gold.
    DietrichUse the special world-map action called 「刃鳴り」 in the Japanese guide to make nearby enemies appear, then fight them.The renown guide identifies these battles as Dietrich’s route-specific source. Selecting the action is not the same as completing the resulting battle.
    TheodoraCheck the items requested by war-preparation tasks and deliver them before the deadline.Deliveries award renown and gold. Check the requested supplies and deadline before planning your gathering.
    LedaUse Leda’s performance activities at taverns and on the world map, including touring performances.The renown guide recommends touring performances as an efficient source. Distinguish these route activities from the shared theatre-performance option in Dagsion.

    Shared sources to check during free time iii

    1. Open the calendar with X to check audience dates. In upper Dagsion, visit a temple highlighted in yellow and speak with its god. An available audience awards renown without spending a turn. If audiences are not yet unlocked, continue the story and other renown activities first.
    2. Check the objectives and deadlines of side quests accepted from noticeboards or townspeople, then complete them. Renown and gold are completion rewards, not acceptance rewards, so plan to finish requests along the route you are already travelling.
    3. When paralogues become available, check each one’s acceptance window and requirements. They award gold as well as renown, but do not all unlock in the same chapter. Treat the renown guide’s Chapters 9–10 as a broad introduction, not a universal unlock date; follow the individual schedule.
    4. At renown 8, performing at the entertainment-district theatre in Dagsion becomes another option. Performing grants renown, support and rewards but consumes motivation and prevents watching a play that week. If motivation recovery is urgent, choose between watching and performing before committing.

    What higher renown actually enables iiii

    Higher renown also affects checkpoint access, world-map feasts, inn menus, training effectiveness, new lessons and temple audiences. The fixed thresholds confirmed here are the exam and theatre values in the table. Do not assume every other facility or special class unlocks at those same levels.

    TargetUnlock and remaining requirements
    Renown 4Intermediate certification and its 500G seal. Check the chosen class’s requirements and success rate separately.
    Renown 7Watching plays, for support and motivation recovery. The renown-earning performance option is not unlocked yet.
    Renown 8Advanced certification and its 3,000G seal, plus theatre performances. Exam access and passing are separate matters.
    Each recruit’s required renownSatisfies one part of recruitment. The same character may have different renown, support, negotiation items and recruitment availability in another route.

    When higher renown still does not solve the problem iiii

    • If a character will not join, first check whether they are recruitable in the current route, then check support, requested items or gold, and story conditions. Required renown is not the chapter number in which they join.
    • If an exam is available but its success rate is low, check character level and required skills rather than simply farming more renown.
    • A different protagonist does not inherit the previous protagonist’s renown level. Keep it separate from other carryover systems, such as support and achievements, and set new renown targets for the new route.
