    Cai’s lessons and class unlocks

    Find Cai’s instructors, collect lesson rewards, and prepare for certification after unlocking new classes.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide
    1. Start lessons in Cai Part I Chapter 4
    2. Choose an instructor
    3. Renown 7: Caladrius for mounted black magic
    4. Renown 8: Dragoon for flying combat
    5. Renown 10: Troubadour for mounted magical support
    6. From unlocking a class to passing its exam

    Start lessons in Cai Part I Chapter 4 i

    1. Reach Cai Part I Chapter 4, then open the base map with ZR. Look for lesson icons on the map or character list.
    2. Speak to the instructor and choose a lesson. Check the assigned objective: lectures, practical exercises and expedition requests have different tasks.
    3. Complete the objective and report back for your reward. Raise renown to unlock further lessons.

    Renown 7: Caladrius for mounted black magic i

    Complete Castor’s renown-7 lesson in Cai’s route to unlock Caladrius certification. Game8 lists Riding D, Black Magic B and character level 35 as preparation targets. Renown 7 opens the request; level 35 is the recommended character level affecting exam success.

    This cavalry class has movement 6 and doubles black-magic uses through its class ability. It is an option when a frequent black-magic user needs mobility and more casts. Check that character’s Riding and Black Magic early so completing the lesson does not leave certification preparation unfinished.

    Renown 8: Dragoon for flying combat i

    Complete Aurora’s renown-8 lesson in Cai’s route to unlock Dragoon certification. Game8’s targets are level 35, Flying C, and Axe B or Spear B as the alternative skill. Both weapon ranks are not required together; check the displayed success rate before taking the exam.

    Dragoon is a flying class with movement 6 and an extra equipped combat-art slot. Its mastery ability adds 20 hit when the enemy occupies advantageous terrain. Decide who benefits from flight and that accuracy bonus, then prepare their skills rather than unlocking the class without an intended user.

    Renown 10: Troubadour for mounted magical support ii

    Cai’s renown-10 lesson unlocks Troubadour certification. Game8’s class article calls the instructor the “White-haired Demon”; its lesson table names Bertrand. Preparation targets are level 35, Riding D, and White Magic B or Black Magic B as the alternative skill.

    Troubadour has cavalry movement 6, restores 5 additional HP when healing allies with magic, and gains 3 hit with magic equipped. Consider it for a mobile healer or magical support unit. Its mastery ability raises nearby allies’ Dexterity and Speed by 2 until your next phase, so its value extends beyond direct damage.

    Cai’s lesson is not the only unlock method. Game8 also lists the fourth new-product showcase touring request in Leda’s route. The lesson and renown sequence on this page applies to Cai.

    From unlocking a class to passing its exam ii

    The checked sources do not provide each lesson’s enemy layout, requested items or travel route. Follow the objective shown when accepting it; do not assume every lesson works identically or can be repeated without costs or limits.

    1. After the lesson unlocks certification, check the intended character’s level and skills. The targets above improve exam success; lesson completion does not automatically change that character’s class.
    2. Speak to the examiner in northern Dagsion and check the class’s exam and success rate. An Advanced Seal is required; the adjacent vendor sells these for 3,000G from renown 8. Caladrius’s lesson unlock at renown 7 does not mean Advanced Seals are sold at that level.
    3. Each attempt consumes one seal, including failures. Game8 reports that seal consumption is saved, preventing a reset from refunding it. If the displayed chance is low, improve the character’s level or skills before attempting the exam.
