    Cai’s cultivation and harvesting

    Use cultivation, unlocked in Cai’s Part I Chapter 6, to multiply recruitment supplies and animal feed. Learn how to choose a plot, harvest while passing through, replace crops and use fertilizer.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide
    1. Unlock cultivation and choose your crop
    2. Plant at a vacant plot and harvest on your route
    3. Fertilizer, plot capacity and replacing a crop
    4. Grow Dates for Halvin’s recruitment
    5. Put the harvest to use

    Unlock cultivation and choose your crop i

    Cultivation unlocks as you progress through Part I Chapter 6 on Cai’s route. If the command is unavailable, advance that chapter first. It multiplies vegetables or plants you already own, so obtain a specimen of the crop you want to grow.

    Start with a concrete need: Dates for Halvin’s recruitment, a favorite food for a horse you are raising, or crops required by an accepted delivery request. A crop being available to plant does not mean it deserves the same priority as one you currently need.

    Plant at a vacant plot and harvest on your route i

    The same crop continues growing after a harvest, but the plot is not permanent. After several harvests it becomes vacant again; start cultivation there again if you still need the crop.

    1. Select a pentagonal map icon and check that its location information identifies a vacant plot. The guide identifies these as vacant plots.
    2. Move onto the plot, open the cultivation command, and select a vegetable or plant from your supplies. If you want a rare harvest, check the fertilizer option and the displayed appearance rates at this stage.
    3. Advance turns on the world map after planting. The crop is ready once its growth gauge fills a complete cycle. Check the plot’s gauge rather than assuming a fixed number of turns.
    4. Passing through a ready plot collects its harvest automatically. You do not have to end your movement there, so you can collect crops on the way to another destination.

    Fertilizer, plot capacity and replacing a crop i

    SituationWhat to do and why
    Seeking a rare harvestFertilizer used when planting improves the chance of rare vegetables or plants shown with low appearance rates. Check that your desired item is among the possible harvests; fertilizer is not a guarantee.
    Expanding cultivationCapacity increases at renown 7 and 9, reaching seven simultaneous plots at renown 9. Raise renown if the current limit prevents you from planting more.
    Changing cropsStarting cultivation anew on an existing plot changes its crop but resets the growth gauge. If a harvest is close, collect it before replanting to avoid wasting that progress.

    Grow Dates for Halvin’s recruitment i

    On Cai’s route, the Chapter 8 request “Huge, Hairy Beasts” rewards two Dates that you can cultivate. Unlocking cultivation in Chapter 6 does not make that reward available immediately. Game8 reports that Cai cannot access the Dates gathering spots, so do not follow another route’s desert gathering directions.

    Plant the Dates and collect enough harvests to set aside ten for recruitment. Cai also needs support 3 and renown 5 to recruit Halvin. Ten Dates alone do not complete every requirement; prepare the support and renown conditions while your crops grow.

    Dates also serve as ingredients for banquets or rests and as trading-company exchange materials. If recruitment is your priority, reserve the ten required Dates before spending the surplus elsewhere.

    Put the harvest to use i

    • Horse feed: identify a favorite vegetable or plant for the horse you are raising and cultivate it. Favorite foods give a larger friendship increase, helping unlock or strengthen that animal’s riding skills.
    • Deliveries: check the accepted request’s items and quantities, then grow what you lack. Harvesting takes turns, so prepare when you identify a need rather than planting immediately before its deadline.
    • Trading-company exchanges: companies in Dagsion and on the world map exchange crops for tools. Check their requested materials before deciding what to grow, and allow for recruitment, feed and deliveries when spending your harvest.
