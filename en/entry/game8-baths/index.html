    Bath unlocks and Sunday specials

    From renown 8, restore two allies, build support and gain chapter-limited skills. Before spending 500G, check the weekly baths, Sunday specials and who will fight in this chapter.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide
    1. Unlocks and benefits: who should receive the 500G visit?
    2. Plan the visit around your battle preparations
    3. Sunday specials: check the skill family you want

    Unlocks and benefits: who should receive the 500G visit? ii

    Baths unlock at renown 8; renown 9 expands the available baths and visit opportunities. The base price is 500G per visit for both regular and Sunday special baths. Choose two allies to increase support, fully restore HP and spell uses, and recover one motivation segment.

    Bath skills are temporary: they last until the current chapter’s main battle ends. A pair that will fight in that battle, needs recovery after exploration and would benefit from more support can serve several goals in one visit. Full motivation does not make a visit pointless if you want the temporary skills and support.

    The motivation guide describes bathing as a recovery option that does not consume a turn. Do not confuse one motivation segment for two selected allies with full recovery for the entire party. If everyone is exhausted, compare this focused visit with party-wide recovery options.

    Plan the visit around your battle preparations i

    1. Check that renown is at least 8. If baths are still locked, first complete renown-granting activities on your route.
    2. Check the bath entry in the calendar for this week’s selection. The lineup changes weekly, so do not assume your preferred bath is always available.
    3. Choose two allies who will fight in this chapter and decide what you need. Compare the current baths if recovery after exploration is urgent; check Sunday’s SPECIAL baths first if special skills are your main goal.
    4. Inspect the skill descriptions and remaining visits in-game before paying 500G. The tables below identify example skills by bath; use the selection screen for values, activation conditions and the complete effects actually offered.
    5. After bathing, assign the restored allies to their next activities and account for the temporary skills when preparing for this chapter’s battle. Do not plan the next chapter around retaining those effects.

    Sunday specials: check the skill family you want i

    SPECIAL baths are available on Sunday. Game8 presents them as an option for stronger skills and recommends Sunday visits. The + below is part of the source’s skill name; it does not establish an exact bonus or multiplier. Bath and skill names are editorial translations.

    Use the role needed in the coming battle to narrow down the listed skill families. An ally seeking Strength/Critical skills will compare different specials from one seeking Magic skills. This is a way to identify candidates, not a claim that one bath is best for every ally.

    Limits and cautions when planning repeat visits ii

    The dedicated bath guide reports four visits Monday–Saturday plus two on Sunday at renown 9, totaling six per week. Its recovery section nevertheless says “up to five,” while the separate motivation guide’s overview table says three per week. The sources do not explain whether these reflect different renown levels or a per-character recovery limit. They cannot be combined into one settled rule. Check the actual calendar and facility counter before planning training and recovery.

    The checked sources do not establish that repeated special baths stack every temporary skill or extend the effect into the next chapter. Another visit uses an available visit and 500G, so inspect current skills and the selection screen before repeatedly bathing an ally who already has the desired effect.
