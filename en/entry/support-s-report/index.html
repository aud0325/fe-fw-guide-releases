    S conversations and ending partner selection

    How to raise support with Eshmel and prepare for ending partner selection.

    In this guide
    1. Support level and conversation rank
    2. Raising support with Eshmel
    3. Choose one partner at the Part 3 ending · Spoilers
    4. S scenes and replay · Spoilers

    Support level and conversation rank

    The numeric support level measures a pair’s relationship; C, B, A and S are conversation ranks. Conversations can also have timing or prerequisite requirements. Check the pair’s conditions in the character’s support table.

    Raising support with Eshmel

    1. Use the bond-deepening interaction at the white raven’s perch. It raises support with Eshmel in bird form and is available once per week.
    2. After interacting, check support between Eshmel and the intended partner. Support between two other allies is a separate relationship.
    Choose one partner at the Part 3 ending · Spoilers
    1. Check that the pair has an A conversation with Eshmel and aim for Support Lv5 with that partner. The minimum level for each partner’s S scene is unknown.
    2. Choose one partner at the Part 3 ending to unlock an S scene. Reaching Support Lv5 during the story does not open the ending scene immediately.
    S scenes and replay · Spoilers

    S scenes may or may not be romantic. The S rank alone does not mean marriage or romance. Unlocked scenes can be replayed in the temple’s Obelisk Chamber.
