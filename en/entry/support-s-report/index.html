    S supports and paired endings (spoilers)

    Separate support level from conversation rank, build your relationship with Eshmel, and check the reported requirements for an S scene and paired ending. The ending conditions come from a community translation of IGN and are folded into a spoiler section.

    In this guide
    1. Support level and A/S conversation ranks are different
    2. Building the relationship with Eshmel
    3. Reported partner selection at the Part III ending · Spoilers
    4. What an S scene means, and the limits of this report · Spoilers

    Support level and A/S conversation ranks are different ii

    Support level measures the relationship between two characters; A and S label support conversations. Reaching level 5 does not by itself mean that an S conversation opens with every character. Check both which pair the level belongs to and which conversations that pair can have.

    The ending report on this page concerns Eshmel and another character. An A conversation between two other allies is not evidence that Eshmel has met the reported condition with either of them.

    Building the relationship with Eshmel i

    1. At the white raven’s perch, check the action for deepening your bond with the raven. Game8 identifies it as a way to raise support with Eshmel in bird form, available once per week.
    2. After interacting, check the support level between Eshmel and your intended partner. Do not confuse progress made between another pair through gifts, meals or other activities with progress in Eshmel’s relationship.
    3. Check newly available support conversations as you progress. Game8 explains that conversations between a pair become available as support increases, but this source does not give the precise thresholds for each of Eshmel’s scenes or a complete ending procedure.
    Reported partner selection at the Part III ending · Spoilers
    i

    The available evidence is a Korean community post translating an IGN guide, with attached images. It reports that at the Part III ending you can choose one partner who can have an A conversation with Eshmel and has reached support level 5, unlocking an S scene. The attached translation presents eligibility as an assessment at the time of writing, not a condition table tested across every route and character.

    • Check the correct pair: Eshmel and your intended partner, rather than two other allies.
    • Reported conversation condition: that partner must be able to have an A conversation with Eshmel. Do not assume that every character is eligible.
    • Reported level and timing: support level 5 between the pair, followed by choosing one partner at the Part III ending. Reaching 5 during the story is not a promise of an immediately available S scene.
    What an S scene means, and the limits of this report · Spoilers
    i

    The attached IGN translation says that an S scene may or may not be romantic. The letter S alone does not guarantee marriage or a romantic ending. It also reports replaying unlocked scenes in the temple’s Obelisk Chamber, but does not establish every eligible partner, the exact selection interface or the preceding story branches.

    The IGN original could not be opened directly. Treat this page as a report of the relationship to prepare and conditions to check at the ending; it does not establish gender restrictions, mandatory gifts, particular dialogue answers or ending branches absent from that evidence.
