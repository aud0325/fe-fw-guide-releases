    Why this route works

    Battlemaster adds 25 percentage points each to HP and strength growth and permits swords, axes and brawling. Its mastery provides an attack dealing 1.5 times damage. Game8 favors retaining these weapon choices and offensive tools instead of advancing solely because another class is later.

    Tactics & limitations

    Being able to qualify for The Godhand does not make it the recommended upgrade. His growth-related personal skill does not erase weaker resistance and luck. He lacks crest and Blaze effects; the guide also flags damage limitations late in Part I on Hard, so inspect actual stats and enemy defenses.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Follow brawling into Battlemaster. Game8 recommends retaining Battlemaster rather than switching to The Godhand.

    A route recommended by the guide, not a mandatory promotion sequence. Check each class for skill requirements and unlock conditions.
