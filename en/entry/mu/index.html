    Part I training guide

    Gauntlets with strength and speed

    1. BeginnerGladiator
    2. →SpecialtyPugilist
    3. →AdvancedWarrior

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop gauntlets with strength and speed together. Signs of Growth plus Pugilist’s strength, speed and defense growth helps his low starting potential. Gauntlets gain a strength bonus from Lv.20 and strength plus speed from Lv.35.

    Skill availability Lv.20 Warrior's Blood · Lv.35 Warrior's Blood+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Brigand is an intermediate alternative for more strength: 5 percentage points more strength growth, with 5 less speed and defense than Pugilist. Prepare white magic later if you want gauntlets with healing.

    Keep the chosen advanced class in Part II. These master and divine classes are role-based options from Part III, not a sequence to complete.

    1. MasterBattlemaster/War Monk
    2. →DivineThe Eternal/The Godhand

    Choose Battlemaster and The Eternal for offense, or War Monk and The Godhand for gauntlets with white magic.
