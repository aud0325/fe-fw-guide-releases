    Part III exploration and rebuilding

    Organize exploration parties, restore travel facilities, and rebuild training and dining in Part III. Personal observations about level scaling are separated from the practical route.

    Before departure: recruit and organize · Spoilers

    Part III’s second section starts with 36 free-action turns. Speak to returning allies in Dagsion to recruit them. For a character recruited in multiple routes, choose which trained version to bring.

    • At rebuilt facilities, undeployed allies can train and eat; deployed allies must return to base. Prepare your main fighters before leaving.
    • For the 16-turn gate operation, Game8 recommends separate combat and gathering/restoration parties. Destroying the gate or reaching the deadline ends the section, so check unfinished tasks first.
    Use every party’s action · Spoilers

    In the community report’s two-party setup, the turn advances after the second party moves, not immediately after the first. Switch parties and give both a destination or exploration task. The report also describes exchanging members when parties meet, and splitting when there is room.

    Restore travel and supply routes · Spoilers

    Gather materials at exploration spots and bring them to damaged stations, temples or settlements. Materials are shared between parties: the gatherer need not return personally. Restoration takes several turns; explore nearby and come back.

    • Prioritize stations and temples to save travel turns through coaches and divine gates. Some temples also unlock master classes after their follow-up requests.
    • City markets help supply rebuilding materials and exam seals. Check restored markets for missing materials before taking another gathering trip.
    • More parties become available as the story advances, allowing gathering and restoration to proceed together.

    Rebuild training, then dining · Spoilers

    Regional restoration materials differ from capital rebuilding materials. Buy the latter at restored markets: cities/capitals sell three, villages/settlements one. Accept and supply rebuilding requests in the imperial capital. Game8 prioritizes the arena to resume weekly training, then dining for motivation recovery without spending a turn.

    FacilityMaterialsPurpose
    ArenaFirst: 3 / second: 20Resume training
    Dining hallFirst: 2 / second: 5Resume meals
    Capital temple7Improve service effects; service is already available
    Compare scaling with your own training · Spoilers

    The original warning against automatic scaling is personal experience. Its author preferred dungeon training after a manually trained level-37 Catania nearly matched a scaled level-42 version. This does not establish the same result for every character or training history.

    The report also describes changed enemy levels and rewards in Part III dungeons. Reassess familiar dungeons against your current party, and compare actual stats rather than judging scaling by level alone.
