    Training guide

    Accurate archery

    1. AdvancedSniper

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Use existing bow ranks and Hit +20 for ranged offense. Dexterity growth exceeds strength growth, so use combat arts or effective bows against sturdy targets.

    Role choices and adjustments

    Choose Bow Adept for less preparation, or Bow Knight for mobility when riding training is feasible. Favor a route usable in the deployments remaining.
