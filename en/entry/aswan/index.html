    Training guide

    1. Advanced classSniper
    2. Master classBow Knight

    Why this route works

    Bow Knight’s movement 7 expands target and positioning choices. Recruitment is very late, however; without time to train Riding D, going directly to Bow Adept through favored Bow A is the practical alternative.

    Tactics & limitations

    Personal +20 bow accuracy and good dexterity help hits, not low strength or resistance. Accuracy alone does not establish damage or safety. Joining in Part-III segment 5 leaves few deployments, favoring an immediately usable role over a long training plan.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Prepare Riding D for Bow Knight. Bow Adept is an alternative if training time is limited.

    Imported guide and source

    This guide summarizes and translates a recommended route brought over from Game8’s Japanese character guide. Training choices can vary with party composition and your goals. Check skill and unlock conditions on each class page.
