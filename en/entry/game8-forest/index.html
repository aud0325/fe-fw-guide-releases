    Lost forest navigation

    Locate the forest in the southern map, follow blue butterflies through five sections, and collect the fourth-section chest. Understand when its accessory activates before equipping it.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide
    1. Finding the forest
    2. Crossing all five sections
    3. Using the fourth-section reward

    Finding the forest i

    Forest of Confusion is an editorial translation of the Japanese dungeon name. Game8’s detailed map places it northeast of Alecto and northwest of Megaira. Look in the southern part of the world map, then inland between those cities.

    The forest name is provisional. The following directions begin inside the dungeon; the source does not establish its earliest accessible chapter or route-specific unlock requirements.

    Crossing all five sections i

    At a fork, advance along the route beside the blue butterflies. In the source image, they glow blue-white above grass beside the path. Locate that marker before committing to an exit.

    1. Find the butterfly-marked path in the first section and follow it.
    2. Find the butterflies again in the second and third sections.
    3. Open the chest in the fourth section before continuing along the marked route.
    4. Follow the same marker in the fifth section to leave the forest.
    • A wrong path triggers a scene and resets you to the beginning. Restart your section count and follow the butterflies again.

    Using the fourth-section reward ii

    The chest contains the Jade Gale Orb (editorial translation); its official localized name has not been matched. Game8 lists it as a normal accessory with weight 5.

    When attacking with a combat art, it has a 30% activation chance and grants Avoid +20 for that combat. Consider it for a combat-art attacker, but treat the effect as a bonus: it is not a permanent Avoid increase, and surviving a dangerous counterattack should not depend on it activating.
