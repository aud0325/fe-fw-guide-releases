    Part I training guide

    Lance cavalry with a magic-based Blaze Arts option

    1. IntermediateArmored Ornius RiderAfter the Cai route Chapter 5 tutorial
    2. Advanced classBardingerLance B · Riding C

    The default starts after Cai route Chapter 5; use the current class before then. Choose the Caladrius alternative below to develop magic-based arts alongside spells.

    Ranks are promotion preparation targets. Check the recruited class, equipment and ranks, and skip earlier stages. There is no need to restart. Check renown, permits and unlocks on the class page.

    Why this route works

    Continuing lance and riding into Bardinger after the Chapter 5 tutorial keeps preparation simple and uses Move +1. Cai also has good strength and magic growth, and his Blaze Arts scale with magic. Caladrius is a worthwhile alternative for combining arts with offensive spells.

    Tactics & limitations

    Black Magic rank prepares Caladrius certification and spell use; it is separate from the magic stat that determines art damage. Check remaining HP and the gauge before using ranged or area arts. Bursting reduces maximum HP, and bow and flying are weaknesses.

    If you want to adjust growth or change roles

    Caladrius offers mobile physical attacks, black magic and magic-scaling arts. Use arena training or manual items to prepare Black Magic B, and check Riding D as well. Obstacle-crossing movement and personal Move +1 help with attack positioning. An established sword build can still remain sword infantry.

    1. IntermediateArmored Ornius RiderAfter the Cai route Chapter 5 tutorial
    2. Advanced classCaladriusRiding D · Black Magic B · Cai route unlock required

    Caladrius: complete Castor’s lesson, Father’s Secret Request, available at Cai route Renown 7. Prepare Riding D and Black Magic B.

    Directions for Parts II and III

    In Part II, continue the main weapon, magic and role chosen in Part I. These master and divine classes are options after their Part III unlocks, not a sequence you must complete.

    Lance cavalry continues into Orichaldia. High Savant is a later candidate for continuing mixed physical and magical offense from Caladrius, with growth bonuses to both strength and magic. It requires the Salvation segment 3 unlock, so prepare for the role you choose.

    • Master classOrichaldia

      Riding B · Lance A or Sword A

      Unlocked after completing Part III Salvation segment 1

    • Divine classThe Cavalier

      Riding A · Sword S or Lance S or Axe S

      From Part III Salvation segment 4. Obtain the class-change item at the Crown Temple; the Crown Key is also needed. · Class-change item: Divine Iron Saddle

    The listed ranks improve exam chances; they do not guarantee passing. Prepare the divine class’s S-rank target, any additional skill rank and its class-change item together.

    Recommendation basis and sources

    The guide uses growth rates, personal abilities, skill strengths, class conditions and documented starting equipment and ranks. A baseline profile does not guarantee the same state at every recruitment time. Routes and roles are our own AI recommendations; original links are in Sources.
