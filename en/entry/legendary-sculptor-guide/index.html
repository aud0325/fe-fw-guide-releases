    Legacy of a Legendary Sculptor

    Search the four locations for the statues, then deliver them to the quest-giver in Dagsion.

    Four statue locations

    1. 1
      Western grassland

      Statue of Jurah

      Gaura Grassland
    2. 2
      Northern pass

      Statue of Kalla

      Utuna Pass
    3. 3
      Southern lake

      Statue of Yu Phas

      Lake Brontes
    4. 4
      Eastern temple

      Statue of Fortuna

      Solel's Temple

    Use Search at each spot to obtain its statue.

    Delivery and rewards

    Leda’s deadline: November 27. Deliver one of each of the four statues.

    5,000 gold · 100 renown · one manual each for riding, heavy armor and flying
