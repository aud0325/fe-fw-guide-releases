    4. Druid

    Why this route works

    Favored Black Magic helps meet Druid’s requirements. Its 30-point magic and 15-point speed growth bonuses plus doubled black-magic uses strengthen his main offense. Druid retains swords, so his other weapon proficiency remains useful.

    Tactics & limitations

    Switch between swords and magic to suit the target and provide +3 dexterity to adjacent allies. Sword access does not make him a durable frontliner: low HP and defense punish exposure after attacking. Spells remain finite even with extra uses. Part-I recruitment is limited to Theodora’s route.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Follow Black Magic into Druid to improve spell availability and speed.

    A route recommended by the guide, not a mandatory promotion sequence. Check each class for skill requirements and unlock conditions.
