    Part I training guide

    A healer who supports nearby allies

    1. Beginner classDivinerWhite Magic D
    2. IntermediatePriestWhite Magic C
    3. Advanced classBishopWhite Magic B

    Ranks are promotion preparation targets. Check the recruited class, equipment and ranks, and skip earlier stages. There is no need to restart. Check renown, permits and unlocks on the class page.

    Why this route works

    Similar strength and magic and lance, white-magic and flying strengths allow several roles. This route prioritizes a party healing need; adjacent post-combat healing is positioning support independent of the class.

    Tactics & limitations

    Speed is modest. Do not take an unfavorable melee fight just to trigger adjacent healing; check the available healing spells first.

    If you want to adjust growth or change roles

    Guardian can combine lance combat and healing. Check its Dietrich or Theodora route unlock first; otherwise keep the bishop path.

    1. IntermediatePriestPrepare healing-spell ranks separately
    2. Advanced classGuardianLance B · Route unlock required

    Guardian: complete Seeking a Challenger: Il-Ila of the Flying Lance at Dietrich route Renown 6, or all Theodora war preparations through Chapter 9. Check the precise start/end deadline in-game for Theodora. Lance B and healing-spell preparation are separate.

    Directions for Parts II and III

    In Part II, continue the main weapon, magic and role chosen in Part I. These master and divine classes are options after their Part III unlocks, not a sequence you must complete.

    Wiseman serves dedicated healing; Sentinel serves a lance hybrid. The latter adds Lance A, with Lance S needed only if choosing The Trident.

    • Master classWiseman

      White Magic A

      Unlocked after completing Part III Salvation segment 1

    • Divine classThe Avatar

      White Magic S (Black Magic S also qualifies)

      From Part III Salvation segment 4. Obtain the class-change item at the Crown Temple; the Crown Key is also needed. · Class-change item: Lotus of Rebirth

    The listed ranks improve exam chances; they do not guarantee passing. Prepare the divine class’s S-rank target, any additional skill rank and its class-change item together.

    Recommendation basis and sources

    The guide uses growth rates, personal abilities, skill strengths, class conditions and documented starting equipment and ranks. A baseline profile does not guarantee the same state at every recruitment time. Routes and roles are our own AI recommendations; original links are in Sources.

    An AI-generated beginner guide based on growth rates, personal abilities and skill strengths. Training choices can vary with party composition and your goals. Mount-growth optimization and fusion are outside its scope.
