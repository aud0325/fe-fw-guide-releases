    Part I training guide

    Healing and adjacent support

    1. BeginnerDiviner
    2. →SpecialtyPriest
    3. →AdvancedBishop

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop white-magic healing behind the front line. Adjacent allies gain post-combat healing and critical avoidance, making him useful just behind a lance defender. Priest and Bishop develop magic, resistance and luck.

    Skill availability Lv.20 Pray for Safety · Lv.35 Pray for Safety+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Guardian is an alternative when he also needs to fight with lances. It favors strength and defense growth over Bishop; prepare the ranks for his healing spells too.
