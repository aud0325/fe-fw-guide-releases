    Training guide

    1. Advanced classOvate
    2. Master classDruid

    Why this route works

    Druid’s 30-point magic and 15-point speed growth bonuses develop spell damage and follow-ups together. The personal +10 accuracy while a spell is equipped fits this role. Favored White Magic also leaves a healing alternative if the party needs it.

    Tactics & limitations

    Good magic and speed do not remove finite spell uses. Low HP and defense require protection from physical enemies. Availability is limited to the prologue and Part III, so continuous Part-I/II training is not possible.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Game8 favors Black Magic into Druid. White Magic proficiency also allows a healing role.

    Imported guide and source

    This guide summarizes and translates a recommended route brought over from Game8’s Japanese character guide. Training choices can vary with party composition and your goals. Check skill and unlock conditions on each class page.
