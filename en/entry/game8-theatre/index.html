    Theatre unlocks

    Choose watching for motivation or performing for rewards before using the theatre this week. Check unlocks, casting, acting cues and reward tiers here.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide

    2. Deciding how to use this week’s visit

    Finding and unlocking the theatre iii

    In Dagsion, use the base map to locate the entertainment district and approach the receptionist in front of the semicircular theatre. Watching unlocks at renown 7; performing at renown 8. Both operate from 06:00 to 23:59 in game.

    Deciding how to use this week’s visit iiii

    Performing prevents watching that week. Before visiting, consider the motivation you need for training or temple service and whether you need money.

    GoalChoice and reason
    Restore motivation and build a pair’s supportWatch. Choose two characters who like the play for better support gains. A disliked play does not restore motivation.
    Gain group support, renown and goldPerform. Ordinary shows involve three characters; special shows involve five. This costs 1 motivation rather than restoring it.
    Save the opportunity for a special showCheck the calendar before performing. Performances are limited to once a week, so an ordinary show uses that week’s opportunity.

    Watching: match the play to your pair iiii

    Watching costs 500G and is available once a week. It does not consume a turn, allowing you to restore motivation and build support for two companions without advancing free time.

    1. Select watching at reception and choose the two characters whose support you want to develop. They can build support without fighting together.
    2. Check their preferences for the play. Game8’s weekly guide identifies the yellow marker at a character’s upper right as the favorable match. Liked plays increase support gains; disliked plays reduce them.
    3. If recovery is the goal, avoid anyone who dislikes the play. Watching helps after training or temple service, but a disliked play fails to restore motivation even when some support is gained.

    Performing: cast roles and choose cues in time i

    1. At renown 8, check the calendar and opening hours. Check your cast’s remaining motivation before selecting a performance.
    2. Cast companions whose support you want to build, favoring those suited to each role. Unsuitable roles give less support.
    3. Read the play’s emotional sequence below before starting. Choose a cue matching each act within five seconds; timeout selects one of the three options automatically.

    Acting cues by play i

    These are the reported cues for three plays, expressed as emotions because localized wording may differ. Courage means a bold, confident manner; laughter means laughing brightly. Titles are editorial translations. This is not a complete play list.

    PlayEmotions in act order
    Ordinary · Phantom of the Arena1 Courage → 2 Anger → 3 Laughter
    Ordinary · Imperial Capital Shrew Uproar1 Sadness → 2 Anger → 3 Joy
    Special · Koen, Singer of the Age1 Anguish → 2 Love → 3 Courage → 4 Laughter → 5 Sadness

    Rewards and limits i

    Check role suitability and acting cues to aim for a high rating. Ordinary GREAT and PERFECT pay the same gold, but PERFECT adds a gift. Special shows offer more participants and larger rewards, making their dates worth checking.

    • Game8’s reward table includes a gift for special-show GREAT. Gift types and quantities, and special-show BAD gold, are not reported here; do not budget for a specific item.
