    Cross-route carryover

    Separate save-wide achievements and Salvation’s Blessings, support between characters, and the map, side-quest and blessing carryover earned by completing a protagonist’s story. Plan the next route without confusing these benefits with renown.

    In this guide
    1. Switching routes is different from starting after a clear
    2. While a route is ongoing: check achievements and Salvation’s Blessings
    3. What becomes easier after finishing one protagonist
    4. Using inherited support for recruitment
    5. Before restarting a route you have already begun

    Switching routes is different from starting after a clear ii

    Each protagonist can have a separate suspend save. Return to the obelisk chamber from a perch to continue another protagonist’s story. Simply switching between ongoing routes does not grant every benefit associated with completing one. Check the timing in the table before planning around carryover.

    Progress or benefitScope and timingWhat this changes for your plan
    Achievements · Salvation’s BlessingsShared across all save dataCheck accumulated achievements and unlocked bonuses without waiting to finish a route.
    Support levelsSupport between the same characters carries to other routesCheck whether it is the relationship between your new protagonist and the recruit.
    Explored map · completed shared side quests · blessing levelsApply to a playthrough begun after completing a protagonist’s storyThey also apply to a different protagonist. An already-started route can receive this treatment by restarting from Chapter 1.
    RenownDoes not carry to a new protagonistDo not assume your previous renown immediately unlocks recruitment, exams or facilities on the new route.

    While a route is ongoing: check achievements and Salvation’s Blessings i

    Salvation’s Blessings and the blessing levels described below are listed as separate carryover systems. Their similar names do not mean blessing levels immediately synchronize across every save.

    1. Check completed achievements and their rewards. The source describes accumulating achievements to obtain causality fragments; this is separate from rewards that require finishing a protagonist first.
    2. Review the Salvation’s Blessings unlocked with causality fragments. These lasting bonuses can ease leveling and shopping, helping both the current route and later protagonists.
    3. Distinguish unlocking a bonus from enabling it. Salvation’s Blessings can be toggled individually, so you can use selected effects or leave them off to preserve the challenge.

    What becomes easier after finishing one protagonist i

    For example, Dietrich gains renown through encounters triggered by his unique action. On a first playthrough, exploring and fighting those encounters compete for turns. Inheriting an explored map eases that pressure, but it does not inherit the renown itself: you still need to earn renown on the new route.

    • Previously completed shared side quests: the same quests can begin in a completed state and still provide rewards. This saves turns otherwise spent repeating them; it does not automatically complete every quest or an errand you have never finished.
    • Map exploration: inherited exploration makes destinations easier to locate and allows longer trips by shared carriage, reducing the turn pressure of exploration and travel. Still check the current chapter’s schedule and the routes actually available to you.
    • Blessing levels: built-up blessings make combat easier. The source suggests using that breathing room for training and support growth. This does not establish that character levels, equipment or money are all copied as well.

    Using inherited support for recruitment ii

    Support is a relationship between two characters. If you developed support between companions A and B on one route, that same relationship can carry to another and help with collecting support conversations. It does not turn into support between a different protagonist C and B.

    Recruitment checks support between the current protagonist and the target. On a new route, check that route’s support, renown and extra item requirements on the character page, then compare them with your current in-game values. Even sufficient support does not replace the need to earn renown; item requirements or story timing can still prevent immediate recruitment.

    Before restarting a route you have already begun ii

    If you have not started the next route, completing one protagonist at a time is a straightforward way to benefit from carryover. If several routes are already underway, the source says to restart another route from Chapter 1 after finishing one protagonist to treat it as a subsequent playthrough. This is different from continuing its existing suspend save.

    The source-supported scope of this guide is the set of systems in the table. It is not an exhaustive inventory of money, weapons, character stats or the synchronization timing of ongoing saves. Do not restart solely on the assumption that a particular item or trained unit will transfer.

    1. Check the current chapter and suspend point first, and decide whether you are willing to lose the progress made within that chapter. Restarting for carryover benefits still means reopening an earlier chapter.
    2. In the obelisk chamber, review that protagonist’s chapter records and restart from Chapter 1. Resuming an earlier chapter discards that route’s existing suspend data; do not treat this as opening a harmless extra save slot.
    3. After restarting, check the map, completed shared quests and blessings, then set fresh renown targets for the new protagonist. Later chapter-start records can also be overwritten as you complete chapters in the new run, so decide what you want to preserve before progressing.
