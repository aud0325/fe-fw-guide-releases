    Role & recommended training i

    1. Wing Soldier
    2. Shido
    3. Swordmaster

    Why this route works

    Training Sword in Wing Soldier leads to Shido and Swordmaster for Cleansing Blade access, a 20-point speed growth bonus and +7 sword critical. Game8 favors its finishing potential over retaining flight in Celestial Trooper: the tradeoff is flight versus sword offense.

    Tactics & limitations

    Each adjacent ally adds +5 accuracy and evasion, so formation matters. Low strength, HP and defense punish isolation or failed evasion. Joining from Part-I chapter 7 onward also limits early training; class bonuses do not provide a finished attacker immediately.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Train Sword in Wing Soldier before Shido. Celestial Trooper is an alternative, but Game8 favors Swordmaster, unlocked in Salvation segment 3.

    A route recommended by the guide, not a mandatory promotion sequence. Check each class for skill requirements and unlock conditions.
