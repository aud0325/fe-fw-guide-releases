    Training guide

    1. Advanced classWarrior
    2. Master classBattlemaster
    3. Divine classThe Eternal

    Why this route works

    He joins as Warrior, so extending existing Axe and Brawling training is practical. Battlemaster uses all three favored weapons and is available before Swordmaster’s Salvation-segment-3 unlock. The same investment remains useful in The Eternal.

    Tactics & limitations

    The +50 accuracy or +30 evasion arts cost 6 HP, allow an attack afterward and last until the next player phase. Physical or magical defensive buffs provide other matchup options. Low base resistance and a personal skill with a 5% trigger make deliberate buff selection important.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Continue from the joining class, Warrior, into Battlemaster to use his sword, axe and brawling strengths.

    Imported guide and source

    This guide summarizes and translates a recommended route brought over from Game8’s Japanese character guide. Training choices can vary with party composition and your goals. Check skill and unlock conditions on each class page.
