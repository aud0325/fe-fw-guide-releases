    Capture unlock and bait selection

    After unlocking capture in Cai Part I Chapter 5, match a location and bait, capture an animal, assign it as a mount, and raise its friendship.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    Start with the unlock, a location, and suitable bait i

    Capture and stable care unlock during Cai Part I Chapter 5. If capture is unavailable, progress to that point. Begin on a world-map tile with an animal icon; care for captured animals at the stable in lower Dagsion.

    Each animal has a preferred food category and flavor. Check the target animal’s appearance information, then obtain suitable food before setting out. A location can list more than one animal: suitable bait does not mean that you have successfully captured the particular animal you wanted.

    Luring, capturing, and recovering from a failed attempt i

    1. Move to a world-map tile with an animal icon and check whether its appearance information includes your target.
    2. Place a preferred food when luring. Game8 reports that unsuitable food causes an encounter battle instead of attracting an animal. If capture is your goal, avoid food that would trigger a battle.
    3. Let several turns pass after placing the bait. Return to the tile when an animal appears and select the capture command from the wait menu or another available menu. The source does not specify a fixed number of turns.
    4. The capture attempt can fail. If it fails, or a different animal appears, place suitable bait at the same location again. Treat bait placement, waiting for an appearance, and capture as separate stages rather than one guaranteed success.

    Some mounts come with a recruited companion ii

    Rocinan joins with Io, and Bucephalus joins with Alexandra. If either is your goal, check that companion’s recruitment conditions for your current route before searching for a wild capture point. Game8’s acquisition table also lists Alexandra’s recruitment instead of a capture location for Bucephalus.

    Assign the mount and develop its friendship i

    After capture, assign the animal to a character through deployment in the preparation menu (身支度 in the Japanese guide). Game8 reports that an assigned mount provides stat bonuses and riding skills, and that riding also increases growth rates. Check the selected animal’s displayed bonuses and skills because effects differ between animals.

    Feed the animal at the lower-city stable to raise friendship. Check its preferred food category, flavor, and favorite food; a favorite food is an efficient choice when available. One feeding makes the animal full, so you cannot keep feeding it consecutively. Advance turns and feed it again when it is hungry.

    Game8 reports that friendship improves stat and growth bonuses as well as riding-skill effects, and also recommends deploying the animal in battle to develop friendship. Do not assume one universal growth-rate bonus or a fixed feeding interval: this source supplies neither.

    How horse skills change at friendship 3 and 5 ii

    Food is useful beyond attracting a new animal: it also develops the skills of a horse you already ride. Namu Wiki lists separate effects at three and five hearts. The examples below apply to cavalry; check the selected animal’s description rather than assuming the same skills apply to every species or class.

    Wild Horse increases reach; Ferghanan Horse also helps reposition after combat. Monoceros heals its rider after combat, so that healing cannot keep the rider alive through a fight they otherwise would not survive. Game8 likewise recommends Wild Horse for improved movement and Monoceros for stronger healing.

    Choosing which animal to develop first i

    For Light Cavalry and Bardinger, Game8 recommends Wild Horse and Monoceros. Wild Horse’s movement skill improves from +1 to +2 after strengthening, helping the rider reach distant enemies. Monoceros’s self-healing skill improves from a small to a moderate heal, helping recover HP spent on Blaze Arts.

    Choose the role you need: develop Wild Horse for reach, or Monoceros to help with HP costs. These are recommendations for those classes, not a ranking for every class. Healing is not unlimited; check current HP and the actual skill description before using an HP-consuming art.

    Renaming and deciding whether to release an animal i

    You can name or rename an animal at the stable. Keep animals you intend to use easy to identify while developing their friendship. Releasing an animal gives a thank-you item based on its friendship at that time. Before doing so, check whether it is one you have assigned or invested in. The source provides neither a complete reward table nor a way to recover a released individual.

    Leave Part III animals for the appropriate stage · Spoilers
    ii

    Namu Wiki places wild capture of Black Horse, Red Bau, and Black Bau from Part III onward. Game8’s complete list combines Cai and Salvation appearances, so inclusion in that list does not mean you should search the early-game map for them. Prepare after checking the appearance information on your current map.

    Using Game8’s food and location information: Black Horse needs bitter vegetables at Deimos Plateau; Red Bau needs spicy meat at Yashiro Fort; Black Bau needs bitter meat at Warrior’s Road or Sidem Valley. Namu Wiki’s Black Horse table has an incomplete name and skill label and no flavor value; it is not a basis for assigning another horse’s skills to Black Horse.
