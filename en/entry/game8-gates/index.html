    Divine gates and travel

    Find each protagonist’s gate unlock and travel from branch shrine to main temple without spending turns or gold. In Part III, restore damaged temples first.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    When your route’s gate opens i

    Story progress unlocks the gate associated with your protagonist. This is a connection to the corresponding temple, not unrestricted travel to every shrine.

    ProtagonistTempleUnlock
    LedaKallaChapter 4
    DietrichMarsChapter 5
    CaiAuroraChapter 6
    TheodoraYufasChapter 12

    Branch shrine to main temple ii

    The branch shrine is the temple facility at your base; the main temple is its counterpart on the world map. For example, Leda reaches Kalla’s main temple through the branch shrine in upper Dagsion.

    1. After unlocking your route’s gate, enter the corresponding branch shrine.
    2. Interact with the divine gate inside and select its main temple as the destination.
    3. Carry out your business at the destination. For Kalla’s bow trial, choose the trial at the main temple’s service reception. It opens after Chapter 10 begins, later than the gate itself.

    Use the free connection to save turns i

    Gate travel costs no turns or gold. For business near a temple, consider the gate before walking the entire route; saved turns can go toward training or requests.

    Theodora: the gate to Chapter 12’s destination i

    Theodora’s Yufas gate opens after the opening event of Chapter 12, followed by 32 free-action turns. At the deadline, take the temple’s gate and follow the destination marker. Do not plan around having this travel option during earlier chapters.

    Part III: restoration and master-class unlocks · Spoilers
    i

    Damaged temples in Part III require restoration before reuse. Check for temples and stations near your destination to shorten later journeys. Free gate travel does not remove restoration materials or waiting time.

    1. Gather the required materials at exploration spots and supply the damaged temple. Materials are shared across parties.
    2. Restoration takes several turns. Check your remaining time, explore nearby, then return.
    3. Check the restored temple’s travel facility and requests. For its master-class unlock, complete the relevant request as well as restoring the temple.
