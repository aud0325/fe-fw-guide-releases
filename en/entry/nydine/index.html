    Part I training guide

    Breakthrough cavalry

    1. BeginnerOrnius Rider
    2. →SpecialtyArmored Ornius Rider
    3. →AdvancedBardinger

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop cavalry mobility and breakthrough. Reduced terrain costs and passing through enemies help reach targets or rejoin allies. Continue riding from Ornius Rider toward Bardinger.

    Skill availability Lv.20 At a Gallop · Lv.35 At a Gallop+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Choose Brigand and Warrior when axe power matters more than mobility. Bardinger retains cavalry skills and resistance growth; Warrior favors strength growth.
