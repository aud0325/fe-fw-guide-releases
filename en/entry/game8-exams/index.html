    Certification and seal stock

    Unlock certification, buy the right seal, check the pass rate, and change class. Separate renown gates from character requirements and prepare before spending limited seals.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide
    1. If exams are unavailable, check the unlock first
    2. Taking an exam in Dagsion
    3. What to improve when the pass rate is low
    4. Seal prices, unlocks, and stock
    5. After failing or passing

    If exams are unavailable, check the unlock first ii

    The first certification unlocks through the story in Theodora’s chapter 4 or Cai, Dietrich, and Leda’s chapter 5. Standard intermediate classes require renown 4; standard advanced classes require renown 8. Some classes have separate unlock conditions, so reaching that renown does not reveal every class.

    Renown opens exam tiers; character level and skill ranks affect the chance of passing. An unavailable exam and an available exam with a low pass rate need different preparation.

    Taking an exam in Dagsion i

    The certification examiner is in northern Dagsion. The seal seller beside the examiner sells the seals needed for each exam tier.

    1. Speak to the examiner and inspect the available classes. Compare the target class’s recommended level and skill requirements with the character you want to certify.
    2. Prepare the matching seal. Each attempt costs one seal, so count the attempts needed for every character you plan to certify.
    3. Check the displayed pass rate before committing. Below 100%, weigh the benefit of changing class now against losing the seal; if there is no urgency, improve the missing level or skills first.
    4. Taking the exam spends the seal. Passing grants that class; use the class-change menu described below to check the result.

    What to improve when the pass rate is low i

    Game8 reports a 100% pass rate when both the recommended character level and all required skill ranks are met. A small skill shortfall can still leave a 100% chance, so a missing rank alone need not stop you. Use the rate shown for that character and exam.

    For advanced classes, being below the recommended level of 35 can sharply reduce the chance even with strong skills. The following are examples recorded by Game8, not a formula that predicts every character’s result.

    Observed casePreparationDisplayed chance
    Theodora Lv37 · WarriorBrawling two ranks short100%
    Lilian Lv33 · SniperSufficient skills, but two levels below the recommendation32%

    Seal prices, unlocks, and stock iii

    These renown values unlock seal tiers. The beginner seal’s renown-1 listing does not replace the story unlock needed to access certification for the first time.

    • Stock is limited and replenishes when you finish the chapter’s main quest and advance to the next chapter. If many allies need certification, buy the seals you need before moving on.
    • If the examiner’s seller is sold out, check markets and merchants at other world-map bases. Availability varies; do not assume every merchant carries every tier.

    After failing or passing iii

    Failure does not return the seal. Game8 reports that the game saves when an attempt consumes a seal, so do not enter on the assumption that restarting will undo the cost. Improve the missing levels or skills and check the rate again to conserve seals and stock.

    A class you have passed can be selected from the class-change menu, including the examiner’s class-change menu. To return to a class you already earned, check that menu rather than starting another exam.

    Can beginner exams skip failed attempts? ii

    One community author reported failure → failure → success when Peter, at Lv17 with an 18% Archer pass rate, took consecutive intermediate exams. The author proposed using two beginner attempts on that character before the intermediate exam to reach the successful result. This came from one person’s save-and-reload experiment; it does not mean every character passes on a third attempt.

    The same author said Cai passed a different exam with a displayed 32% chance before Peter still failed twice. The report therefore does not support using another ally’s attempts to consume the target character’s failures. The author also left unresolved whether outcomes are set at the start of the game or the chapter.

    For actual seal spending, follow Game8’s account of attempts and saving: the seal is consumed and the game saves when you attempt an exam, and failure does not refund it. The community post gives no reproducible procedure for previewing the sequence while avoiding that save. Do not count repeated beginner attempts as a saving strategy when you do not know the successful result. Check the target class’s displayed chance and improve levels or skills before attempting it to manage costs and stock.
