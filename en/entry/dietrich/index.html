    Part I training guide

    Fast sword offense

    1. BeginnerGladiator
    2. →SpecialtyMyrmidon
    3. →AdvancedShido

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Develop fast, precise sword offense with critical potential. Myrmidon and Shido reinforce his high speed and dexterity growth and suit his initiating critical bonus. Use him to finish targets across several fights.

    Skill availability Lv.5 Bestial Impulse · During Overblaze Murderous Intent+

    Role choices and adjustments

    Use Brigand for the intermediate stage when sword damage falls short. Strength growth rises from 45% to 60%, while speed falls from 65% to 55%; return to Shido after developing strength.
