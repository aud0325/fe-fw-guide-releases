    Training guide

    1. Master classWar Monk

    Why this route works

    War Monk combines brawling weapons and white magic, allowing offense and healing in one slot. It offers +7 brawling evasion and doubled white-magic uses, while retaining his personal +10 accuracy with brawling weapons equipped.

    Tactics & limitations

    Strength and speed support brawling follow-ups, but low HP, defense and resistance limit sustained exposure. Healing access does not make him a universal tank. Availability only in the prologue and Part III also limits training opportunities.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Retain the joining War Monk class to keep both gauntlets and white magic.

    Imported guide and source

    This guide summarizes and translates a recommended route brought over from Game8’s Japanese character guide. Training choices can vary with party composition and your goals. Check skill and unlock conditions on each class page.
