    Earning gold and choosing theatre activities

    Choose income sources for your route, budget for theatre motivation and quest deadlines, and preserve useful items while funding training and recruitment.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide

    Decide what the money must cover ii

    The following is a budgeting recommendation. Training, weapons, gifts and recovery all draw on the same funds. Set aside this week’s promotion and recruitment costs before optional purchases. Chasing small gathering profits can cost more if it makes you miss a quest deadline.

    1. Check quest deadlines and the paralogue acceptance windows for your current route; schedule the nearest deadlines first.
    2. Combine route-specific tasks with exploration and battles along the same journey. Use the route table below to choose a source.
    3. On returning, sell surplus after reserving required items, then make optional purchases only after allowing for training and recovery.

    How each protagonist earns gold iii

    Leda’s touring performances and tavern deliveries are distinct. Leaf rewards from touring requests supply tavern tasks that unlock arts and drinks. The money guide’s brief delivery wording does not describe every performance. The shared capital theatre option at renown 8 is another separate activity.

    RouteWhat to doCosts and limits
    CaiAccept favors from townspeople marked with a black hand symbol, check their objectives, and complete them alongside exploration. Ordinary side quests use a central + symbol.Check the deadlines on both favors and ordinary quests. Pearl drops from woodlouse enemies in the sewers provide extra income, but defeated dungeon enemies do not respawn until the next chapter.
    DietrichUse the special world-map action called 「刃鳴り」 in the Japanese guide to spawn nearby enemies, then sell unneeded battle drops while also training your units.Spawning enemies does not itself award sale items. Do not budget a guaranteed payout from every battle; retain funds for recovery and weapon upkeep.
    TheodoraCollect the items requested by military supply tasks and deliver them before their deadlines. Dispatch troops at world-map exploration and dungeon spots to gather materials; sell only the surplus.Reserve the required delivery quantities first. Selling the whole haul can force you to gather the same materials again.
    LedaTake performance requests from the tavern owner, then travel to the glowing musical-note destinations on the world map. More requests appear as chapters progress.Check the deadline and route. A gate on the journey may require more renown; complete other requests or performances if access is blocked.

    At the theatre, choose income or recovery iiiii

    Visit the reception in front of the semicircular theatre in Dagsion’s entertainment district. Watching unlocks at renown 7 and performing at renown 8. You cannot do both in the same week, so check your motivation and funds before choosing.

    If performing will force you to pay for lodging to recover motivation, include that cost in the decision. If you already have enough gold but your next trainees lack motivation, watching may serve you better. Choose for the resource you need this week.

    ChoiceBenefitsCost and trade-off
    PerformGold according to the rating, support between participants, and renown. PERFECT awards 1,000G plus a gift for an ordinary show, or 3,000G plus a gift for a special show.Consumes 1 motivation and gives up watching that week. You can perform once weekly; lower ratings pay less.
    WatchSupport and motivation recovery for two characters, with better support gains when the play suits their tastes.Costs a base 500G and gives up that week’s performance income. Consider it when you need motivation recovery without spending a turn.

    Combine exploration, paralogues and inn work ii

    • Paralogues offer substantial rewards, but their acceptance windows depend on the route. Game8 lists 20,000G each for Bertrand’s and Cai’s paralogues as examples. Do not assume every paralogue pays the same amount; check which ones occur on your route and when you can accept them.
    • Collect enemy drops and chests during dungeon trips. Treat world-map gathering spots as extra income along your route; a long detour solely for low-value gold dust may not be worthwhile.
    • Choose exploration in the inn’s automated activities when you want gold and materials. Rewards accumulate with elapsed turns rather than providing instant cash. Keep upgrade materials you still need.
    • Gold Mummies in encounters can provide bullion, but their appearance is uncertain. Treat them as a bonus while levelling, rather than your guaranteed source for an urgent quest or training expense.

    Reserve recruitment, delivery and upgrade items before selling iiii

    An unequipped item or spare ingredient is not automatically disposable. Reserve recruitment requests, accepted deliveries and planned upgrade materials before selling surplus.

    • For example, Halvin requests 10 Dates in negotiations. Keep them while working toward his recruitment even if you have no immediate use for them; this avoids having to grow or gather replacements.
    • Theodora’s supply materials and the leaf rewards from Leda’s tours can be delivery requirements. Compare them with your active requests rather than judging only by sale price.
    • Keep money for weapon upkeep and training. Training costs half as much on Saturday and Sunday, and meals cost half as much on Sunday; use those discounts when deadlines allow.
    Lasting discounts across routes · Spoilers
    i

    At the white raven’s perch, open Salvation’s Blessings and check weapon, tool and gift purchase discounts. These effects are shared with other protagonists, making them useful for repeated spending across routes. On entering the war arc, check discounts for lodging, meals, theatre visits, baths, the forge and shared carriages as well. These blessings are separate from renown unlocks; check the current price and unlock requirement before purchasing.
