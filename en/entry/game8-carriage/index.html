    Stagecoach unlocks and routes

    Unlock stagecoaches, register stations and choose the correct eastern or western network to save travel turns for distant requests and paralogues.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide

    2. Unlock the feature, then register stations
    3. Eastern and western networks
    4. Spend gold to preserve your remaining turns

    When coaches become available i

    Cai unlocks coaches later: in Part I Chapter 7, complete the favor “Something Unavailable Even in the Imperial Capital”. Travel to Megaira, obtain the requested goods and deliver them. Reaching Chapter 7 alone does not complete the unlock.

    Dietrich, Theodora and Leda unlock the feature automatically when free time begins in Part I Chapter 4. Plan Cai’s early travel on foot, then prioritize this favor in Chapter 7 to save turns on later trips.

    Unlock the feature, then register stations i

    Unlocking coaches does not register every destination. Each station must be visited once before it can be used. Register stations during ordinary exploration instead of leaving the first trip until a distant request is nearly due.

    1. Find the station near your destination and check which network serves it.
    2. Visit unregistered stations in person. To depart, go to a station spot on the world map.
    3. Select the stagecoach at the station, check the available destination and required gold, then travel.
    4. Continue from the arrival station to the actual destination; reaching the station does not complete the request.

    Eastern and western networks i

    East and West Amalthea stations near Dagsion serve separate networks. Travel within a network is possible, but a coach cannot cross directly from the eastern network to a western station. If a destination is missing, check whether it is unvisited or on the other network. Station names below are editorial renderings of the Japanese names.

    Station near DagsionConnected stations
    East AmaltheaCarmenta, Feronia, Urca, Eohid
    West AmaltheaCallisto, Salacia, Chiron, Aragona, Cartodo

    Spend gold to preserve your remaining turns iii

    Fares increase with distance, and insufficient gold prevents travel. Check the fare before leaving and reserve money for the return journey instead of spending everything on equipment or exam items.

    Game8’s Cai Chapter 7 guide describes a coach trip between stations as taking one turn. It is not turn-free teleportation: budget for reaching the departure station, the ride, and the remaining journey to the request.

    Registered stations are particularly valuable near paralogue deadlines. Use the saved turns to finish time-sensitive tasks first, then spend confirmed spare time on shrines, exploration or training. Avoid leaving station registration and fundraising until the deadline.

    Restoring access later in the story · Spoilers
    i

    Damaged stations in Part III’s Salvation arc must be restored before reuse. Gather the required materials at exploration spots and start restoration at the damaged station. Materials are shared across parties, so one can gather while another restores.

    Restoration takes several turns. Explore nearby while it finishes, and include that wait in your return schedule. Restoring stations early saves turns on repeated journeys, especially when revisiting distant areas.
