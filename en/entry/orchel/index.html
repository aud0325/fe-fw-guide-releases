    Training guide

    1. Advanced classBishop
    2. Master classWiseman
    3. Divine classThe Avatar

    Why this route works

    Wiseman combines doubled white magic, +15 healing and axes, using his defense and resistance for tanking and healing. The Avatar triples spell uses and adds +10 magic accuracy to offset low dexterity, while restoring black magic that Wiseman cannot use.

    Tactics & limitations

    Very low speed and dexterity limit follow-ups and axe accuracy; armored treatment also prevents cavalry or flying promotion. His range-1–10 art helps with reach, but requires the once-per-map Overblaze activation and lasts only while its gauge remains. Normal combat with a cursed treasure does not change his gauge.

    Preparation & alternatives

    Proceed from Bishop to Wiseman. Wiseman cannot use black magic; The Avatar restores that option later.
