    Training guide

    White-magic healing and physical defense

    1. AdvancedBishop

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Use white-magic healing alongside high physical defense. Bishop’s ally heals also restore his HP, and initiating spells gain Hit +20. Very low speed favors supporting the front line rather than chasing targets.

    Skill availability Lv.20 Harmonic Soul · Lv.35 Harmonic Soul+ · Initially known Predictive Sense · Acquisition unconfirmed Self-Repair

    Role choices and adjustments

    Prepare The Avatar to use black magic too. Mounted and flying classes are unavailable, so expand spell choices and uses rather than changing to a mobility role.
