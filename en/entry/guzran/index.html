    Part I training guide

    A fast swordsman with an optional strength detour

    1. Beginner classGladiatorSword D
    2. IntermediateMyrmidonSword C
    3. Advanced classShidoSword B

    Ranks are promotion preparation targets. Check the recruited class, equipment and ranks, and skip earlier stages. There is no need to restart. Check renown, permits and unlocks on the class page.

    Why this route works

    Listed Level 5 Gladiator, Bronze Sword, Sword D and Infantry D support continued swords into Myrmidon and Shido. Gauntlet D allows an alternative; holding Axe D does not itself imply an axe strength.

    Tactics & limitations

    Hotheaded does not let you choose which accuracy, avoidance or critical bonus activates. Low resistance calls for avoiding magical counters.

    If you want to adjust growth or change roles

    Use Brigand only for the intermediate stage when strength is limiting damage. Sword C qualifies; its strength modifier is higher and speed modifier lower than Myrmidon. Keep the default when damage is sufficient.

    1. Beginner classGladiatorSword D
    2. IntermediateBrigandSword C or Axe C
    3. Advanced classShidoSword B
    Intermediate growth modifiers (percentage points)
    ClassStrengthSpeed
    Myrmidon+0+15
    Brigand+15+5
    If you want a gauntlet role

    Gladiator → Pugilist → Warrior uses the gauntlet strength. Choose it for the party’s attack needs rather than treating a sword build as a failure.

    1. Beginner classGladiatorGauntlet D
    2. IntermediatePugilistGauntlet C
    3. Advanced classWarriorGauntlet B
    Directions for Parts II and III

    In Part II, continue the main weapon, magic and role chosen in Part I. These master and divine classes are options after their Part III unlocks, not a sequence you must complete.

    Swords point toward Swordmaster and The Blade; gauntlets toward Battlemaster and The Eternal. The Godhand adds Gauntlet S and White Magic C, a weakness.

    • Master classSwordmaster

      Sword A

      Complete Mars’s Request after restoring Mars Shrine in Part III Salvation segment 3.

    • Divine classThe Blade

      Sword S

      From Part III Salvation segment 4. Obtain the class-change item at the Crown Temple; the Crown Key is also needed. · Class-change item: Demonic Blade Guard

    • Master classBattlemaster

      Gauntlet A (Sword A or Axe A also qualifies)

      Unlocked after completing Part III Salvation segment 1

    • Divine classThe Eternal

      Gauntlet S (Sword S or Axe S also qualifies)

      From Part III Salvation segment 4. Obtain the class-change item at the Crown Temple; the Crown Key is also needed. · Class-change item: Scales of Judgment

    • Divine classThe Godhand

      Gauntlet S · White Magic C

      From Part III Salvation segment 4. Obtain the class-change item at the Crown Temple; the Crown Key is also needed. · Class-change item: Emperor’s Sash

      Godhand preparation includes White Magic C as well as Gauntlet S. If white magic is a weakness, Eternal is another way to continue gauntlet offense.

    The listed ranks improve exam chances; they do not guarantee passing. Prepare the divine class’s S-rank target, any additional skill rank and its class-change item together.

    Recommendation basis and sources

    The guide uses growth rates, personal abilities, skill strengths, class conditions and documented starting equipment and ranks. A baseline profile does not guarantee the same state at every recruitment time. Routes and roles are our own AI recommendations; original links are in Sources.
