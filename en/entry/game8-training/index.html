    Skill training and spectating

    Plan weekly training around class and weapon requirements, then use spectating and inn training to fill the remaining skill EXP gap.

    Secondary reporting from Game8 JP, not independently verified in-game. New guide titles and summaries are editorial translations.

    In this guide
    1. Choose the skill you actually need
    2. Four arena training types and how to prepare
    3. Manage weekly limits and weekend discounts
    4. Check the event time before spectating
    5. When weekly sessions are spent or a rank is still short

    Choose the skill you actually need ii

    Skill rank is separate from character level. It unlocks skills and combat arts and improves certification success rates or helps meet weapon requirements. Check the requirements of the class or weapon you want next, then give training priority to characters who are missing those ranks.

    Skill EXP alone does not satisfy every class-change requirement. Some exams allow attempts below the recommended skill ranks, and character level also affects the success rate. Check the displayed rate and required seal at the exam screen. Use the table below to select the training that develops the skills you need.

    Four arena training types and how to prepare ii

    Game8 places the training unlock after clearing Part 1, Chapter 4. Speak to the female receptionist in front of the Grand Arena between 06:00 and 23:59. A session can train up to two characters.

    1. Choose the characters and the ranks they need. For example, if a character needs both Spear and Riding, cavalry and flying training develops both.
    2. Check the selected characters’ motivation and your gold. Top up low motivation with a meal or gift first. Lodging fully restores everyone for 400G and one turn; choose it for a depleted party after checking the time remaining.
    3. Visit the Grand Arena during reception hours and select the training and up to two participants. Check that this training type has not already been used that week.

    Manage weekly limits and weekend discounts i

    Each of the four training types is available once per week. Changing participants does not give you another session of an already-used type that week. Completing infantry training still leaves the other unused types, such as magic training, available.

    Training costs half as much on Saturday and Sunday. If class preparation is not urgent, grouping your sessions on the weekend saves gold. The discount changes the price; it does not add sessions to an already-used weekly allowance.

    Higher renown increases the skill EXP gained from training. Once the week’s sessions are used, use the alternative methods below during your remaining free time instead of repeatedly returning to the receptionist.

    Check the event time before spectating i

    Spectating awards skill EXP to the whole party without consuming motivation or gold. Treat it as an extra opportunity on event days alongside the focused two-character training sessions.

    The event window is short, so check the receptionist or the calendar’s events for its date and time when planning free time. Spectating is not available throughout the arena’s 06:00–23:59 training hours. Check whether you can return in time before committing to an expedition or other activities.

    When weekly sessions are spent or a rank is still short i

    • Choose Training in the inn’s delegated activity to gain class EXP and skill EXP. Exploration gives gold and materials, while Practice gives character EXP and class EXP, so choose the activity that matches your goal.
    • Dungeon battles and encounters award skill EXP according to the weapons and classes used. If you can already use a weapon required by your target class, fight with it to work toward the missing rank.
    • A manual grants its listed skill EXP to the character who uses it. Check the recipient and the manual’s skill against the intended class or weapon requirement before consuming it; a manual does not train unrelated skills.
    Training during the war and beyond · Spoilers
    i

    Regular arena training is unavailable during the war arc. The army action for training all skills grants skill EXP to all allies, but only once per chapter. Game8 describes the reward as roughly one normal training session, so do not plan around repeating it without limit.

    Skill EXP bonuses from Salvation’s Blessings apply across activities that grant the relevant skill EXP and carry forward to the next protagonist after finishing a story. Use weekly sessions for immediate class preparation and consider these lasting bonuses when planning several routes.
