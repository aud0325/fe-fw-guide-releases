    Training guide

    Gauntlet combat arts

    1. MasterBattlemaster

    Continue from the recruited class and ranks. Select a class name for its promotion requirements.

    Why this route works

    Keep recruited Battlemaster and focus on gauntlet arts. High HP and strength growth support strong hits, with a chance for extra follow-ups through his skill. Allies holding targets in place help him contribute.

    Role choices and adjustments

    Prioritize gauntlet hit and combat arts over new weapons. Low speed favors attacking alongside another front-liner rather than holding several enemies alone.
